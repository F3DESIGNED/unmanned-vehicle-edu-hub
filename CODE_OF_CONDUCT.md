# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on advancing unmanned systems
education for all learners.

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and educational outcomes
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Acknowledging and respecting the diverse backgrounds of educators and students
- Prioritizing safety and responsible practices in all shared content
- Supporting and encouraging learners at all skill levels
- Sharing knowledge generously while giving credit to others' work
- Maintaining patience when helping others troubleshoot problems

### Examples of unacceptable behavior

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Dismissing or belittling others' questions or contributions
- Deliberately sharing unsafe practices or encouraging dangerous behaviors
- Promoting commercial interests without community benefit or transparency
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting

## Educational Community Standards

As an education-focused project, we hold additional standards:

### Safety First

- **Never compromise safety** - All shared content must prioritize the safety of students and educators
- **Age-appropriate guidance** - Consider the developmental level of learners when contributing
- **Regulatory compliance** - Respect and promote compliance with aviation and safety regulations
- **Risk awareness** - Acknowledge hazards and provide appropriate safety guidance

### Educational Excellence

- **Pedagogical soundness** - Content should reflect sound educational practices
- **Accessibility** - Strive to make resources accessible to diverse learners and contexts
- **Standards alignment** - When possible, align with recognized educational standards
- **Evidence-based** - Support recommendations with research or documented experience

### Professional Conduct

- **Maintain professionalism** - Remember students and educators may be reading
- **Protect student privacy** - Never share identifying information about students
- **Respect intellectual property** - Properly attribute sources and respect copyright
- **Avoid conflicts of interest** - Disclose commercial relationships when relevant

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
Examples of representing our community include:

- Using an official project e-mail address
- Posting via an official social media account
- Acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event
- Contributing to the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Participating in community forums or chat channels

This Code of Conduct also applies to behavior outside the community when that
behavior has the potential to adversely affect the safety and well-being of
community members, particularly students.

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at:

**Email:** education@f3designed.com

**For urgent safety concerns:** Contact your local authorities first, then notify us.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident.

### Confidentiality

We will respect confidentiality requests for the purpose of protecting victims of abuse. We may publicly name a person about whom we've received harassment complaints only when:

1. We've received explicit consent from the reporter to do so, OR
2. The behavior is clearly and unambiguously a safety threat to other community members

### Reporter Protection

We will not ask reporters to confront the person who has allegedly violated the Code of Conduct. We will not reveal the identity of reporters without their consent, except in cases where we believe there is a threat to others' safety.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This
includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels
like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent
ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or
private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction
with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period.
Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
community.

## Safety-Specific Violations

Given our educational focus, the following may result in immediate temporary or permanent ban:

- Deliberately sharing unsafe practices that endanger students or educators
- Encouraging violation of aviation or safety regulations
- Promoting dangerous modifications without appropriate safety warnings
- Sharing content that enables harm to others
- Attempting to circumvent safety features or regulations

## Appeal Process

If you believe you have been wrongly accused of violating this Code of Conduct, you may appeal by:

1. Sending a detailed explanation to education@f3designed.com within 14 days
2. Include any relevant context or evidence
3. A different community leader will review your appeal
4. You will receive a response within 21 days
