# Professional Learning Community (PLC)

Ongoing professional development and collaborative learning for educators teaching unmanned vehicle systems.

## What is the PLC?

A structured professional learning community providing:
- Monthly professional challenges
- Book studies and discussion groups
- Webinar series
- Certification pathways
- Conference resources
- Peer collaboration

## Monthly Challenges

**Professional growth through practice**

Each month, take on a challenge designed to expand your skills and improve your teaching.

- [Challenge Archive](monthly-challenges/challenge-archive.md)
- [2026 Challenges](monthly-challenges/2026/) - Full year calendar
- Earn badges and recognition
- Connect with others doing same challenges
- Build portfolio of work

**See:** [Community Monthly Challenges](../../docs/community/events/monthly-challenges/challenge-calendar-2026.md)

## PLC Activities

### Lesson Study Cycles
Collaborative lesson development and refinement
- Plan together
- Observe teaching
- Reflect and improve
- Share refined lessons

### Action Research
Investigate questions from your practice
- Develop research questions
- Collect data
- Analyze findings
- Share with community

### Curriculum Development Sprints
Intensive collaborative curriculum creation
- Weekend or week-long
- Team-based
- Produce complete units
- Share openly

### Peer Observation
Learn from watching and being watched
- Virtual or in-person
- Non-evaluative
- Structured protocols
- Reflection and discussion
