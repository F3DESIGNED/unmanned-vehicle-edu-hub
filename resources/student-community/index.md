## Community Guidelines for Students

**Be respectful:**
- Kind to others
- Constructive feedback
- Celebrate successes
- Support when someone struggles

**Be safe:**
- Follow safety rules always
- Ask before posting photos of others
- Protect your personal information
- Report concerns to adults

**Be honest:**
- Give credit for ideas
- Don't copy others' work
- Admit when you don't know
- Learn from mistakes

**Be awesome:**
- Share what you learn
- Help others
- Try new things
- Have fun!

## Parent/Guardian Information

**For parents:**
- All student participation requires parent/guardian permission
- Community is moderated by educators
- Safety and privacy are priorities
- Students learn valuable technical and collaboration skills
- Great for college applications and resumes

## Getting Involved

**Ready to jump in?**

1. **Get permission** from parent/guardian and teacher
2. **Read** [Getting Started for Students](getting-started-students.md)
3. **Introduce yourself** in Discussions (first name and grade only)
4. **Explore** resources that interest you
5. **Share** your first project or question
6. **Connect** with other students
7. **Have fun** learning and building!
