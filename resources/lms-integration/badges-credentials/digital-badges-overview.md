# Digital Badges Overview

Digital badges recognize skills and accomplishments, providing portable credentials students can share.

## Benefits
- Portable credentials
- Skill-specific recognition
- Shareable on social media/resumes
- Motivation for students
- Detailed skill evidence

## Badge Platforms

### Badgr (by Instructure)
- Free for educators
- Canvas integration
- Industry standard (Open Badges)

### Credly
- Professional credentials
- Employer recognition
- Analytics

### Custom Badges
- Create your own
- School-branded
- Flexible criteria
