# State Grants Overview

Most states offer STEM and Career & Technical Education (CTE) grants that can support drone programs.

## Types of State Grants

### CTE Equipment Grants
- Modernizing labs and equipment
- Typically $5,000-$50,000
- Annual cycles

### STEM Education Grants
- Innovative STEM programs
- Varies by state
- Competitive

### Workforce Development
- Programs aligned with industry needs
- Can be substantial
- Often require industry partnership

## How to Find State Opportunities

1. State Department of Education website
2. State Economic Development office
3. Governor's workforce initiatives
4. Lieutenant Governor education programs

## General Tips

- Align with state workforce priorities
- Emphasize industry partnerships
- Show connection to jobs
- Include matching funds if possible
