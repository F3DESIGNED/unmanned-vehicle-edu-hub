# Crowdfunding Guide for Drone Education Programs

## Why Crowdfunding?

**Advantages**:
- Direct community engagement
- Quick funding for specific needs
- Builds program awareness
- No complex applications

**Best for**:
- Equipment purchases
- Specific projects
- Program launches
- Supplemental funding

## Major Platforms

### DonorsChoose (Education-specific)
- **Focus**: Public school projects
- **Fee**: ~15% (includes shipping/handling)
- **Timeline**: 4 months to fundraise
- **Best for**: Classroom equipment and materials

### GoFundMe (General)
- **Focus**: Any cause
- **Fee**: ~3% + payment processing
- **Timeline**: Flexible
- **Best for**: Community-based programs, clubs

### Kickstarter (Project-based)
- **Focus**: Creative projects
- **Fee**: 5% + payment processing
- **Timeline**: 30-60 day campaigns
- **All-or-nothing**: Must reach goal
- **Best for**: Innovative curriculum or tools
