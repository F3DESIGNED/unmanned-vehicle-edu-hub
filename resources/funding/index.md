## Funding Sources Overview

### Federal Grants ($5,000 - $100,000+)
Competitive but substantial funding for STEM education

**Best for**: Established programs, experienced grant writers
**Timeline**: 3-6 months from application to award
**See**: [Federal grants directory](./grant-opportunities/federal-grants/)

### State Grants ($2,000 - $50,000)
State-specific STEM and CTE funding

**Best for**: Programs aligned with state workforce needs
**Timeline**: 2-4 months
**See**: [State grants overview](./grant-opportunities/state-grants/)

### Foundation Grants ($1,000 - $25,000)
Private foundations supporting education

**Best for**: Specific projects with clear outcomes
**Timeline**: 1-3 months
**See**: [Foundation grants](./grant-opportunities/foundation-grants/)

### Corporate Sponsorships ($500 - $10,000)
Company support for programs

**Best for**: Local businesses, industry partners
**Timeline**: Ongoing throughout year
**See**: [Corporate sponsorships](./grant-opportunities/corporate-sponsorships/)

### Crowdfunding ($500 - $5,000)
Online community support

**Best for**: Specific equipment needs, new programs
**Timeline**: 30-60 day campaigns
**See**: [Crowdfunding guide](./grant-opportunities/crowdfunding/)

## Program Budgeting

### Budget Scenarios by Program Size

| Budget Level | Drones | Students | Staff | Annual Operating Cost |
|-------------|--------|----------|-------|---------------------|
| **Micro** (<$5K) | 2-3 | 10-15 | 0.25 FTE | $1,000-$2,000 |
| **Small** ($5-15K) | 4-8 | 20-40 | 0.5-1 FTE | $3,000-$6,000 |
| **Medium** ($15-50K) | 10-20 | 50-100 | 1-2 FTE | $8,000-$15,000 |
| **Large** ($50K+) | 25+ | 100+ | 2+ FTE | $20,000+ |

See [budget scenarios](./program-budgeting/budget-scenarios/) for detailed breakdowns.

### Cost Categories

**One-Time Startup Costs**:
- Drones and batteries: $300-$3,000 per drone
- Safety equipment: $200-$500
- Tools and charging: $200-$500
- Curriculum materials: $100-$500
- Initial training: $500-$2,000

**Annual Operating Costs**:
- Replacement parts: 15-20% of drone costs
- Battery replacements: $100-$500/year
- Insurance: $500-$2,000/year
- Consumables: $200-$500/year
- Professional development: $300-$1,000/year

**Optional Costs**:
- Competition fees: $500-$2,000
- Travel: $500-$5,000
- Advanced equipment: $1,000-$10,000+
- Expanded facilities: Varies widely

See [startup costs calculator](./program-budgeting/startup-costs-calculator.xlsx) for detailed estimates.

### Grant Writing Timeline

**8-12 weeks before deadline**:
- Identify opportunity and confirm eligibility
- Form writing team
- Begin needs assessment

**6-8 weeks before**:
- Complete needs assessment
- Draft goals and objectives
- Outline methods and activities
- Begin budget development

**4-6 weeks before**:
- Complete first draft of narrative
- Finalize budget
- Request letters of support
- Identify evaluation methods

**2-4 weeks before**:
- Revise based on feedback
- Finalize all components
- Gather required attachments
- Have others proofread

**1 week before**:
- Final review and polish
- Submit early (don't wait for deadline!)
- Confirm receipt
- Celebrate submission!

## Sustainability Planning

### Five-Year Sustainability Model

**Year 1**: Grant-funded startup
- Focus on implementation
- Build student interest
- Document successes
- Begin revenue exploration

**Year 2**: Continued grant support + initial revenue
- Expand program
- Start service learning projects
- Build community partnerships
- Introduce fundraising

**Year 3**: Mixed funding model
- Reduce grant dependence
- Grow revenue streams
- Establish donor base
- Institutionalize program

**Year 4**: Primarily self-sustaining
- Grants for expansion only
- Multiple revenue streams
- Strong donor support
- Budget line item established

**Year 5**: Sustainable operations
- School budget integration
- Diversified funding
- Growth capacity
- Model for others

## Common Funding Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge 1: "No grant writing experience"
**Solutions**:
- Use detailed templates provided
- Partner with experienced grant writer
- Start with small, local grants
- Take grant writing workshop
- Learn from successful examples

### Challenge 2: "Grants are too competitive"
**Solutions**:
- Focus on good fit, not prestige
- Tell compelling story with data
- Demonstrate clear need
- Show strong support
- Start smaller and build track record

### Challenge 3: "Takes too much time"
**Solutions**:
- Use templates to save time
- Form writing team to distribute work
- Reuse components for multiple grants
- Build proposal library over time
- View as investment in program

### Challenge 4: "Limited budget makes us uncompetitive"
**Solutions**:
- Start with micro-budget grants
- Leverage in-kind contributions
- Show creative resourcefulness
- Partner with others to pool resources
- Focus on outcomes per dollar

### Challenge 5: "Grant ends, program ends"
**Solutions**:
- Plan sustainability from day one
- Develop multiple funding streams
- Institutionalize program (school budget)
- Build community support
- Create revenue opportunities

## Expert Tips

### From Successful Grant Recipients

**"Start early"**: Don't wait until a month before the deadline. Give yourself time to write, revise, and gather support materials.

**"Tell stories with data"**: Numbers are important, but personal stories of student impact make proposals memorable.

**"Build relationships"**: Get to know program officers. Ask questions. Show genuine interest in the funder's mission.

**"Follow instructions exactly"**: Grant reviewers look for reasons to eliminate proposals. Don't give them one by ignoring requirements.

**"Plan sustainability from day one"**: Funders want to see their investment will continue. Show how the program will sustain beyond the grant period.

**"Learn from rejection"**: Request reviewer feedback and use it to strengthen future proposals. Most successful grant writers have faced rejection.
