# Grant Writing Basics

Complete guide to writing successful grant proposals for drone education programs.

## Understanding Grants

### What is a Grant?
A grant is money given by an organization (foundation, government, corporation) to support a specific project or program. Unlike loans, grants don't need to be repaid.

### Types of Grants

**Project Grants**: Fund specific initiatives
**Equipment Grants**: Purchase hardware/materials
**Program Grants**: Support ongoing programs
**Capacity Building**: Organizational development
**Planning Grants**: Develop proposals or programs

### Grant vs. Other Funding

| Type | Repay? | Application | Control | Best For |
|------|--------|-------------|---------|----------|
| Grant | No | Complex | Funder oversight | Established programs |
| Donation | No | Simple | High | Any size |
| Loan | Yes | Moderate | High | Equipment purchase |
| Sponsorship | No | Pitch | Negotiable | Events, partnerships |

## Key Components of Grant Proposals

### 1. Executive Summary
- Brief overview (1 page)
- Write this LAST
- Hits all key points
- Compelling and clear

### 2. Need Statement
- What problem are you solving?
- Who is affected?
- Data supporting the need
- Why now?

### 3. Goals and Objectives
- **Goals**: Broad outcomes
- **Objectives**: Specific, measurable targets (SMART)
- Aligned with funder's priorities

### 4. Methods/Activities
- What you'll do
- Timeline
- Who's responsible
- Resources needed

### 5. Evaluation
- How you'll measure success
- Assessment tools
- Reporting frequency
- Data collection methods

### 6. Budget
- Detailed line items
- Justification for each
- In-kind contributions
- Sustainability plan

### 7. Organization Qualifications
- Your track record
- Staff expertise
- Facilities and resources
- Past successes

### 8. Sustainability
- How program continues after grant
- Other funding sources
- Revenue generation
- Institutionalization
