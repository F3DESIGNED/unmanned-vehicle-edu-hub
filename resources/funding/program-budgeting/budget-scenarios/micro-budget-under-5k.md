# Micro Budget: Under $5,000

Detailed budget scenario for starting a drone program with minimal funding.

## Budget Overview

**Total Startup**: $3,500
**Annual Operating**: $1,200
**Students Served**: 10-15
**Program Model**: After-school club or elective

## Detailed Startup Budget

### Equipment ($2,400)
- 3× DJI Tello EDU drones @ $150 = $450
- 6× Extra batteries @ $20 = $120
- 3× Charging hubs @ $30 = $90
- Prop replacements (bulk) = $50
- 2× Tablets for control @ $200 = $400
- Safety glasses (class set) = $100
- Carrying cases = $90
- Landing pads and markers = $100
- **Subtotal: $1,400**

**Equipment Notes**:
- Start with DJI Tello (most affordable programmable option)
- Educational discount available
- Good indoor/outdoor performance
- Python programming capable

### Safety & Facilities ($300)
- First aid kit = $50
- Fire extinguisher = $50
- Safety cones/boundaries = $100
- Storage cabinet (repurposed) = $0
- Signage = $100
- **Subtotal: $300**

### Curriculum & Materials ($200)
- Printed materials = $50
- Online resources (free) = $0
- Software licenses (free/open source) = $0
- Obstacle materials (DIY) = $100
- Challenge props = $50
- **Subtotal: $200**

### Insurance ($600)
- Liability insurance year 1 = $600
- (May be covered by school policy)

### Training & Professional Development ($500)
- Teacher Part 107 prep course = $200
- Part 107 exam fee = $180
- Online PD courses (free/low cost) = $100
- Conference (virtual) = $20
- **Subtotal: $500**

### Miscellaneous ($500)
- Contingency fund = $500
- (For unexpected needs)

**TOTAL STARTUP: $2,500-$3,500**
(Range depends on insurance coverage)

## Year 1 Operating Budget

### Consumables ($400)
- Replacement props = $100
- Batteries (replacements) = $150
- Tape, markers, supplies = $50
- Printing = $100

### Equipment Maintenance ($300)
- Minor repairs = $200
- Calibration tools = $100

### Professional Development ($200)
- Part 107 renewal (year 2) = $0
- Workshop attendance = $100
- Online courses = $100

### Competition/Events ($300)
- Virtual competition entry = $50
- End-of-year showcase = $150
- Community demo day = $100

**TOTAL ANNUAL: $1,200**

## Funding Strategy

### Initial Funding
**Target: $3,500**

**Option 1: Single Grant**
- Local foundation grant ($3,000)
- DonorsChoose campaign ($500)

**Option 2: Multiple Sources**
- Parent organization ($1,000)
- Local business sponsorship ($1,000)
- DonorsChoose ($1,000)
- School allocation ($500)

**Option 3: Bootstrapping**
- Use school tablets (save $400)
- DIY cases and storage (save $200)
- Virtual training (save $300)
- Start smaller → Start with $2,600

### Sustainability Plan

**Year 1**: Grant-funded startup, prove concept
**Year 2**: Mix of school budget ($600) + fundraising ($600)
**Year 3**: School budget line item ($1,200)

## Program Implementation

### Timeline

**Summer Before Launch**:
- Secure funding
- Purchase equipment
- Teacher obtains Part 107
- Setup curriculum

**Fall Semester**:
- Recruit students (10-15)
- Foundations (8 weeks)
- Intermediate skills (8 weeks)

**Spring Semester**:
- Advanced projects (8 weeks)
- Competition preparation (4 weeks)
- Showcase event (1 week)
- Reflection (1 week)

### Space Requirements

**Classroom**:
- Regular classroom size adequate
- Storage for equipment
- Internet access

**Flying Space**:
- Gymnasium (indoor)
- Parking lot (outdoor)
- Field or open space

### Staffing

**Teacher Time**:
- 0.25 FTE (one section)
- After-school club: 3 hours/week
- Or elective: 1 period daily

**Support**:
- Volunteer mentor (industry) - 2 hours/month
- Student officers - built into program

## Student Experience

### What Students Get

**Equipment Access**:
- Shared drones (3-5 students per drone)
- Personal controller access (tablets)
- All safety equipment

**Curriculum**:
- Complete foundational curriculum
- Programming basics
- Safety certification
- Project portfolio

**Opportunities**:
- Virtual competition participation
- Community demonstration
- Portfolio for college
- Potential Part 107 prep (advanced students)

### Program Limits

With micro budget, cannot provide:
- Individual drones for each student
- Advanced equipment (FPV, etc.)
- Travel to in-person competitions
- Extensive field trips
- Paid guest speakers

But can still provide high-quality foundational education!

## Expansion Path

### Growing from Micro to Small Budget

**Year 2 Goals** ($5,000-$7,000):
- Add 2-3 more drones
- Attend regional competition
- Upgrade to intermediate platform (1-2 drones)
- Expand to 20-25 students

**Year 3 Goals** ($8,000-$12,000):
- Multiple sections or full course
- Better equipment diversity
- Competition travel
- Guest speakers

## Success Metrics

Track to demonstrate value:

**Participation**:
- 10-15 students enrolled
- 80%+ completion rate
- 50%+ female participation (goal)
- Diverse demographics

**Outcomes**:
- Safety: Zero accidents
- Skills: All complete portfolio
- Interest: 80%+ want to continue
- Certification: 2-3 students earn Part 107

**Impact**:
- Community awareness events (2-3)
- Media coverage (1-2 stories)
- Administrator satisfaction
- Student testimonials

## Real Program Example

**Jefferson Middle School, Small Town, USA**

**Budget**: $3,200 (local grant)
**Students**: 12 (6th-8th grade after-school club)
**Outcome**: 100% completion, 1 student won state science fair with drone project, program funded by school in year 2

**Key Success Factors**:
- Engaged teacher with passion
- Strong safety record
- Community demonstrations
- Parent support and volunteer help
