# Flight Operations Skills Checklist

## Purpose

This performance-based assessment evaluates student competency in safe drone flight operations. Students demonstrate skills through a practical flight test while the evaluator observes and rates performance.

**Assessment Type:** Performance-based observation
**Time Required:** 15-20 minutes per student
**Setting:** Outdoor flying area or indoor flight space
**Equipment:** Drone, controller, flight area, safety equipment

---

## Administration Instructions

### Preparation

**Before assessment:**
1. Set up safe flying area with clear boundaries
2. Ensure equipment is functional and charged
3. Review assessment criteria with students in advance
4. Provide practice opportunities before formal assessment

**Student briefing:**
- Explain assessment process and expectations
- Review safety protocols
- Allow questions
- Emphasize safety over speed

**Evaluator role:**
- Observe objectively using this checklist
- Take notes on specific observations
- Prioritize safety (stop assessment if unsafe)
- Provide encouraging, constructive feedback

## Proficiency Levels

**Based on total score (out of 60 points):**

### Advanced (54-60 points / 90-100%)
- Demonstrates mastery of all flight operations
- Executes maneuvers smoothly and precisely
- Consistently maintains safety protocols
- Shows confidence and situational awareness
- **Recommendation:** Ready for advanced flight challenges, autonomous programming

### Proficient (48-53 points / 80-89%)
- Demonstrates solid competency in flight operations
- Executes maneuvers successfully with minor imperfections
- Follows safety protocols consistently
- Shows good situational awareness
- **Recommendation:** Ready for more complex flight tasks, may benefit from practice on advanced skills

### Developing (36-47 points / 60-79%)
- Demonstrates basic flight competency
- Completes required maneuvers with some difficulty
- Generally follows safety protocols
- Needs improvement in precision and consistency
- **Recommendation:** Needs additional practice before advanced tasks, focus on specific weak areas

### Beginning (Below 36 points / Below 60%)
- Limited flight competency
- Struggles with basic maneuvers
- Safety concerns present
- Needs significant support
- **Recommendation:** Requires substantial additional instruction and practice before independent flight

## Notes for Evaluators

**Best Practices:**
- Create a supportive, low-stress environment
- Focus on safety first, performance second
- Allow students to self-correct minor errors
- Take detailed notes for feedback
- Provide immediate, constructive feedback after assessment
- Recognize improvement and effort

**Common Issues and Solutions:**
- **Nervousness:** Normalize pre-flight jitters, allow warm-up practice
- **Equipment problems:** Have backup equipment ready
- **Weather changes:** Be flexible with scheduling, prioritize safety
- **Failure:** Frame as learning opportunity, create clear remediation plan

**Modifications for Different Levels:**
- **Elementary:** Focus on Parts 1-3, 6-7 (basic operations and safety)
- **Middle School:** Include Parts 4 (basic maneuvers)
- **High School:** Include all parts including advanced skills
