# UVS Knowledge Assessment - High School (Grades 9-12)

## Test Administration Instructions

**Purpose:** This assessment measures student knowledge of unmanned vehicle systems concepts taught in the curriculum.

**When to administer:**
- **Pre-test:** First week of course, before instruction begins
- **Post-test:** Final week of course, after instruction concludes

**Time:** 40-45 minutes

**Format:** Multiple choice (30 questions)

**Scoring:** 1 point per correct answer (Total: 30 points)

**Instructions to students:**
"This assessment helps us understand what you know about unmanned vehicle systems. Do your best, but don't stress - this is not for a grade. Choose the best answer for each question."

## Scoring Guide

**Raw Score to Percentage:**
- 30 = 100%
- 27-29 = 90-96% (A range)
- 24-26 = 80-86% (B range)
- 21-23 = 70-76% (C range)
- 18-20 = 60-66% (D range)
- <18 = Below 60%

**Proficiency Levels:**
- **Advanced (27-30):** Strong comprehensive understanding
- **Proficient (22-26):** Solid understanding with minor gaps
- **Developing (17-21):** Basic understanding, needs reinforcement
- **Beginning (<17):** Limited understanding, significant instruction needed

## Interpretation and Use

**For Pre-Assessment:**
- Identify baseline knowledge
- Don't expect high scores (that's okay!)
- Use to differentiate instruction
- Identify specific topic areas needing emphasis

**For Post-Assessment:**
- Measure learning gains (compare to pre-test)
- Identify persistent gaps
- Celebrate growth
- Inform future instruction

**For Program Evaluation:**
- Calculate mean pre/post scores and gains
- Analyze by section to identify curriculum strengths/weaknesses
- Disaggregate by student subgroups
- Compare across classes/schools
