# Parent/Guardian Consent Form for Research Participation

## UVS Education Hub Pilot Program - Research and Evaluation

---

## To Parents/Guardians:

Your child has the opportunity to participate in the **Unmanned Vehicle Systems (UVS) Education Hub Pilot Program** at [School Name]. As part of this program, we conduct research and evaluation to understand how students learn and to improve the program for future students.

**This form explains the research and asks for your permission for your child to participate in evaluation activities.**

---

## What is the UVS Pilot Program?

The UVS Education Hub provides curriculum for teaching students about drones, robotics, and unmanned vehicle systems. Your child's teacher is participating in our pilot program to test this curriculum and help us improve it.

**All students in the class will participate in the course and receive instruction.** This consent form is ONLY about research and evaluation activities.

---

## What does the research involve?

If you consent to your child's participation in research, we will collect information about their learning and experiences:

### **Assessments (Required for all pilot schools):**
1. **Knowledge tests** - Multiple choice tests before and after the course (30-40 minutes each)
2. **Skills assessments** - Hands-on demonstrations of drone operation and programming
3. **Attitude surveys** - Questions about interest in STEM and career goals (10-15 minutes each)
4. **Student feedback survey** - Questions about what they liked and learned in the course (10 minutes)

### **Additional research activities (May vary by school):**
- **Student interviews** - Individual or small group discussions about their experiences (20-30 minutes)
- **Classroom observations** - Researchers may observe your child's class (no disruption to instruction)
- **Student work samples** - Photos or copies of projects your child creates
- **Photos/videos** - Images of students learning (see separate photo release form)

**Total time:** Approximately 2-3 hours over the course of the semester

---

## What happens to the information collected?

### **Privacy and Confidentiality:**
- Your child's name will NEVER be used in any reports or publications
- Data will be stored with ID codes, not names
- Only approved researchers will have access to identifiable data
- Published results will use group averages or anonymous quotes
- All data stored securely and destroyed per retention policies

### **FERPA Compliance:**
We will comply with all Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) requirements for protecting student education records.

### **How information will be used:**
- Evaluate the effectiveness of the UVS curriculum
- Improve the program for future students
- Publish research findings in academic journals and reports
- Share results with educators, researchers, and policymakers
- Report to program funders

---

## What are the risks?

**Risks are minimal and similar to everyday school activities:**
- Time and minor inconvenience for assessments and surveys
- Possible discomfort answering questions (students may skip any question)
- Small risk of data breach (minimized through security measures)
- No physical risks beyond normal class activities

**Safety:** All hands-on activities follow safety protocols and are supervised by trained teachers.

---

## What are the benefits?

**Direct benefits to your child:**
- Learn about exciting technology and careers
- Develop valuable STEM skills
- Opportunity to reflect on their learning
- Contribute to improving education

**Benefits to others:**
- Help improve the program for future students
- Advance knowledge about how students learn STEM content
- Inform educational policy and practice

---

## Is participation voluntary?

**YES! Participation in research is completely voluntary.**

**Important:**
- Your child can still take the course even if you do not consent to research
- Students who don't participate in research will do alternative activities during research time
- There is NO penalty for not participating in research
- Your decision will NOT affect your child's grade or standing in the class

**You or your child may withdraw from the research at any time** by notifying the teacher or principal. Data collected before withdrawal may still be used unless you request otherwise.
