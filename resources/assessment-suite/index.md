# Assessment Suite - UVS Education Hub

## Overview

The UVS Education Hub Assessment Suite provides comprehensive tools for measuring student learning, program implementation, and overall impact. These instruments support both formative assessment for learning and summative evaluation for accountability and research.

---

## Purpose

The assessment suite enables:
- **Student learning measurement** - Track knowledge, skills, and attitude changes
- **Program evaluation** - Assess implementation quality and fidelity
- **Continuous improvement** - Inform curriculum and support refinements
- **Research and evidence** - Build rigorous evidence of program impact
- **Accountability** - Demonstrate outcomes to stakeholders and funders

## Assessment Administration Guidelines

### Pre-Assessment (Before Program Start)

**When:** First week of program, before instruction begins

**Administer:**
- Knowledge test (appropriate grade level)
- Baseline skills assessment (if applicable)
- Attitude surveys (STEM interest, self-efficacy, career interest)

**Time required:** Approximately 45-60 minutes total

**Tips:**
- Explain purpose: to understand starting point, not for grades
- Emphasize "do your best" but no pressure
- Allow adequate time, don't rush
- Collect consent/assent forms first

---

### Formative Assessment (During Program)

**Ongoing throughout program:**
- Observation of student work and engagement
- Informal checks for understanding
- Project milestones and checkpoints
- Portfolio entries and reflections

**Mid-program:**
- Interim skills assessments
- Check on portfolio progress
- Informal attitude check-in

**Purpose:** Guide instruction, provide feedback, adjust pacing

---

### Post-Assessment (End of Program)

**When:** Final week of program, after instruction concludes

**Administer:**
- Knowledge test (same version as pre-test)
- Final skills assessments
- Attitude surveys (same as pre-test)
- Program satisfaction survey
- Portfolio evaluation

**Time required:** Approximately 60-90 minutes total

**Tips:**
- Schedule sufficient time, not rushed at semester end
- May spread across multiple days
- Celebrate learning and growth
- Collect student feedback

## Data Management

### Collecting Data

**Best practices:**
- Use consistent identifiers (not names) for matching pre/post
- Keep master key linking IDs to names secure
- Enter data promptly and accurately
- Back up data regularly
- Store securely (encrypted, password-protected)

### Submitting Data to Pilot Program

**Timeline:**
- Pre-assessment data: Within 2 weeks of administration
- Mid-program data: By mid-semester deadline
- Post-assessment data: Within 2 weeks of program completion

**Format:**
- Use provided spreadsheet templates
- De-identified (ID codes only, no names)
- Include complete documentation

**Submit to:** evaluation@uvs-edu-hub.org

## Interpreting Results

### Individual Student Level

**Use assessment data to:**
- Identify learning gains for each student
- Recognize growth in knowledge, skills, and attitudes
- Provide feedback to students and parents
- Guide future learning pathways

**NOT for:**
- High-stakes grading or promotion decisions (pre/post tests)
- Comparing students to each other
- Punitive purposes

---

### Class/Program Level

**Use aggregate data to:**
- Evaluate overall program effectiveness
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement
- Inform curriculum revisions
- Report to stakeholders
- Contribute to research

**Analyses:**
- Pre/post gain scores (t-tests, effect sizes)
- Percentage proficient or showing growth
- Disaggregated by student subgroups
- Correlations with implementation fidelity

## Assessment Quality Standards

**All assessment instruments are:**
- **Aligned** with curriculum objectives and standards
- **Age-appropriate** for target grade levels
- **Reliable** (consistent measurement)
- **Valid** (measure what they claim to measure)
- **Fair** and free from bias
- **Practical** to administer in school settings

**Continuous improvement:**
- Pilot tested and revised based on feedback
- Psychometric properties analyzed
- Updated based on evaluation findings

## Using Assessment Results

### For Teachers

**Formative use:**
- Identify student misconceptions
- Adjust instruction and pacing
- Differentiate for student needs
- Provide targeted feedback

**Summative use:**
- Evaluate overall learning outcomes
- Inform grading (in combination with other evidence)
- Report to students, parents, administrators

---

### For Program Improvement

**How assessment informs program:**
- Which curriculum units are most/least effective?
- What skills do students master? What's challenging?
- How do attitudes change (or not)?
- What implementation factors predict success?
- How do outcomes vary across contexts?

**Leads to:**
- Curriculum revisions
- Enhanced professional development
- Improved support resources
- Refined assessment tools

---

### For Research and Advocacy

**Evidence of impact:**
- Student learning gains
- Engagement and interest in STEM
- Career aspirations
- Implementation effectiveness across contexts

**Publications and reporting:**
- Research papers
- Evaluation reports
- Case studies
- Success stories
