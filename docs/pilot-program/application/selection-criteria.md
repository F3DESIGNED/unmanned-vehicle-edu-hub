# Pilot Program Selection Criteria

## Overview

The UVS Education Hub Pilot Program selection process is designed to identify schools that are well-positioned to successfully implement our curriculum and provide valuable feedback for continuous improvement. We seek diverse schools representing various contexts (urban/suburban/rural, different grade levels, varying resource levels) to ensure our curriculum works across settings.

## Selection Philosophy

We aim to select schools that demonstrate:
- **Readiness**: Capacity and resources to implement effectively
- **Commitment**: Dedication to program fidelity and evaluation participation
- **Diversity**: Varied contexts to test curriculum adaptability
- **Impact potential**: Ability to reach underserved student populations
- **Sustainability**: Plans for continuation beyond pilot phase

## Evaluation Process

Applications are reviewed in three stages:

### Stage 1: Initial Screening (Eligibility)
All applications screened for basic eligibility requirements (pass/fail).

### Stage 2: Scored Review
Eligible applications scored by review team using criteria below.

### Stage 3: Finalist Interviews
Top-scoring applications invited for virtual interviews.

---

## Scoring Rubric

**Total Points: 100**

Applications scored on the following criteria:

### 1. School Context and Need (15 points)

**Geographic and demographic diversity (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Underserved locale (rural/remote) or high-need urban school
- 3 pts: Suburban school serving diverse population
- 1 pt: Well-resourced school with limited diversity

**Student population served (5 points)**
- 5 pts: High percentage (>50%) FRL, significant underrepresented groups
- 3 pts: Moderate diversity (25-50% FRL)
- 1 pt: Low diversity (<25% FRL)

**Program gap/need (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Limited STEM/CTE offerings, program fills clear gap
- 3 pts: Some STEM offerings, program complements existing
- 1 pt: Extensive STEM offerings, program duplicates existing

### 2. Resources and Infrastructure (20 points)

**Adequate funding (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Committed budget >$15,000 from sustainable sources
- 3 pts: Budget $8,000-$15,000, mix of sources
- 1 pt: Budget <$8,000 or uncertain funding
- 0 pts: No identified funding

**Appropriate physical space (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Dedicated lab space with outdoor flying area and secure storage
- 3 pts: Shared space with adequate features OR dedicated without outdoor area
- 1 pt: Minimal space, significant adaptations needed
- 0 pts: No appropriate space identified

**Technology infrastructure (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Excellent internet, WiFi, 1:1 devices or well-equipped lab
- 3 pts: Good connectivity, adequate device access
- 1 pt: Limited connectivity or devices
- 0 pts: Inadequate technology infrastructure

**Existing equipment (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Relevant equipment already available (drones, robotics kits, tools)
- 3 pts: Some equipment, will supplement
- 1 pt: Minimal equipment, will purchase all
- 0 pts: No plan for equipment acquisition

### 3. Teacher Qualifications and Commitment (25 points)

**Relevant background and experience (10 points)**
- 10 pts: STEM/CTE teacher with robotics/programming experience, industry background
- 7 pts: STEM teacher with some technical experience
- 4 pts: Teacher with limited technical background but strong learner
- 1 pt: Non-STEM teacher with minimal technical experience

**Technical skills (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Average self-rating ≥4 across skill areas
- 3 pts: Average self-rating 3-3.9
- 1 pt: Average self-rating 2-2.9
- 0 pts: Average self-rating <2

**Professional development commitment (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Commits to all PD opportunities (required + optional)
- 3 pts: Commits to all required PD
- 1 pt: Commits to required PD with reservations
- 0 pts: Unable to commit to required PD

**FAA Part 107 certification (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Currently holds Part 107 certificate
- 3 pts: Committed to obtaining before program start
- 1 pt: Willing to obtain during program
- 0 pts: No plan to obtain certification

### 4. Administrative and Institutional Support (15 points)

**Administrator support level (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Strong support letter, active advocacy, resource commitment
- 3 pts: Support letter showing approval and encouragement
- 1 pt: Minimal support letter
- 0 pts: No administrator letter submitted

**Institutional alignment (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Program clearly aligns with school/district strategic priorities
- 3 pts: General alignment with STEM/CTE goals
- 1 pt: Tangential alignment
- 0 pts: No clear alignment

**Sustainability commitment (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Clear plan for continuation, expansion, and resource allocation
- 3 pts: General plans for continuation
- 1 pt: Vague sustainability plans
- 0 pts: No plans beyond pilot year

### 5. Program Design and Goals (10 points)

**Clear, measurable objectives (5 points)**
- 5 pts: 3 specific, measurable, achievable objectives aligned with curriculum
- 3 pts: 2-3 objectives, somewhat specific
- 1 pt: Vague or unrealistic objectives
- 0 pts: No clear objectives

**Appropriate implementation plan (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Well-designed course structure, realistic schedule, adequate contact hours
- 3 pts: Reasonable plan with minor concerns
- 1 pt: Questionable feasibility (too short, unrealistic schedule)
- 0 pts: Inadequate or unclear plan

### 6. Assessment and Evaluation Participation (10 points)

**Commitment to data collection (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Commits to all assessment activities, has IRB/consent plan
- 3 pts: Commits to most activities
- 1 pt: Limited commitment
- 0 pts: Unwilling to participate in evaluation

**Reflective practice commitment (5 points)**
- 5 pts: Enthusiastic about reflection, documentation, sharing
- 3 pts: Willing to complete required reflection/documentation
- 1 pt: Minimal commitment to reflection
- 0 pts: Unwilling to engage in reflection

### 7. Student Access and Equity (5 points)

**Recruitment strategy (3 points)**
- 3 pts: Intentional recruitment of underrepresented students, clear strategies
- 2 pts: Open enrollment with some outreach
- 1 pt: Passive recruitment
- 0 pts: Selective enrollment excluding some students

**Support for diverse learners (2 points)**
- 2 pts: Specific accommodations and supports planned
- 1 pt: General commitment to accessibility
- 0 pts: No plan for supporting diverse learners

---

## Minimum Thresholds

To be considered for selection, applications must:
- **Score ≥60 points overall** (out of 100)
- **Score ≥10 points in "Resources and Infrastructure"** (out of 20)
- **Score ≥15 points in "Teacher Qualifications and Commitment"** (out of 25)
- **Include administrator signature and support letter**
- **Commit to required professional development**
- **Commit to program evaluation participation**

---

## Additional Selection Considerations

Beyond scored criteria, the selection committee considers:

### Cohort Diversity
To ensure curriculum works across contexts, we aim for cohort diversity:
- **Geographic**: Mix of urban, suburban, rural, remote schools
- **Grade levels**: Elementary, middle, and high school representation
- **School types**: Public, charter, CTE centers
- **Resource levels**: Mix of well-resourced and under-resourced schools
- **Regional**: Geographic distribution across states/regions

### Timing and Logistics
- **Start date alignment**: Can school start on cohort schedule?
- **Teacher availability**: Can teacher complete summer training?
- **Equipment procurement**: Realistic timeline for setup?

### Special Considerations
- **First-time implementations**: Schools new to unmanned systems (for testing beginners' curriculum)
- **Experienced programs**: Schools with existing programs (for testing advanced pathways)
- **Innovation potential**: Unique approaches or contexts
- **Partnership opportunities**: Strong industry or university connections
- **Scalability**: Potential to influence other schools in district/region

### Red Flags (May Result in Rejection)
- Incomplete application or missing required signatures
- Unrealistic expectations or misalignment with curriculum goals
- Inadequate resources with no plan to acquire
- Teacher unable to commit to required PD
- Lack of administrator support
- Unwilling to participate in evaluation activities
- Concerning student selection practices (overly selective, exclusionary)

## Interview Process (Finalists Only)

Top-scoring applications invited for 45-minute virtual interviews with selection committee.

### Interview Participants
- Lead teacher (required)
- School administrator (required)
- Additional team members (optional)

### Interview Topics
1. **Vision and motivation**: Why this program? What impact do you hope to have?
2. **Implementation logistics**: Walk through your implementation plan
3. **Problem-solving**: How would you handle [scenario-based questions]?
4. **Collaboration**: How will you engage with pilot community?
5. **Equity and access**: How will you ensure all students can succeed?
6. **Sustainability**: Plans beyond pilot year?
7. **Questions for us**: What support do you need?

### Interview Scoring
Each committee member rates interview on 5-point scale:
- 5: Exceptional - clear vision, well-prepared, enthusiastic, collaborative
- 4: Strong - good responses, demonstrates readiness
- 3: Adequate - acceptable responses, some concerns
- 2: Weak - unclear responses, significant concerns
- 1: Poor - not ready for pilot participation

Interviews scoring <3 average typically not selected.

## Final Selection

### Selection Committee
- UVS Education Hub curriculum director
- Lead curriculum developer
- Pilot program coordinator
- External education expert (CTE or STEM specialist)
- Industry partner representative

### Decision Process
1. **Initial scoring**: Each committee member independently scores applications
2. **Calibration meeting**: Discuss discrepancies, align on standards
3. **Final scores**: Committee agrees on final scores
4. **Finalist selection**: Top-scoring applications invited to interview
5. **Interview evaluation**: Committee conducts and scores interviews
6. **Final deliberation**: Consider scores, diversity goals, logistics
7. **Selections**: Identify accepted schools, waitlist, declined
8. **Notification**: All applicants notified of decision

### Timeline
- Applications reviewed within 2 weeks of submission (rolling)
- Finalist interviews scheduled within 4 weeks
- Final decisions within 6 weeks
- Schools notified immediately upon decision

---

## Selection Outcomes

### Accepted
- Receive formal acceptance letter
- Assigned to cohort
- Begin onboarding process
- Receive access to curriculum and support resources

### Waitlist
- May be offered spot if accepted school declines or conditions change
- Encouraged to apply for future cohorts
- Can request feedback for strengthening future application

### Declined
- Receive brief explanation for decision
- Encouraged to apply for future cohorts
- Offered feedback call if desired
- Can access open curriculum resources independently

---

## Appeals Process

Applicants may appeal decisions based on:
- Factual errors in application review
- Procedural irregularities
- New information significantly affecting application

**To appeal:**
1. Submit written appeal to pilot-program@uvs-edu-hub.org within 10 days of decision notification
2. Include specific grounds for appeal and supporting documentation
3. Appeals reviewed by program director within 5 business days
4. Decision on appeal is final
