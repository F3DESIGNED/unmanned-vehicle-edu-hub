## Purpose

## Part I: Pilot Program Commitments to Schools

The UVS Education Hub Pilot Program commits to providing the following support:

### 1. Curriculum and Materials
- ☑ Complete, standards-aligned curriculum for selected grade level(s)
- ☑ Lesson plans, student materials, assessments, and teacher guides
- ☑ Access to online curriculum platform and resource library
- ☑ Regular curriculum updates based on feedback
- ☑ Sample equipment lists and procurement guidance

### 2. Professional Development
- ☑ 2-day intensive summer training institute (all expenses covered except travel)
- ☑ Monthly online learning community meetings (1 hour, recorded)
- ☑ One-on-one coaching sessions (minimum 2 per semester)
- ☑ Access to on-demand PD modules and webinars
- ☑ Optional mid-year workshop (1 day, location TBD)
- ☑ End-of-year reflection retreat (1 day, location TBD)

### 3. Implementation Support
- ☑ Dedicated pilot program coordinator as primary contact
- ☑ Technical assistance (within 24-48 hours response time)
- ☑ Weekly check-in emails/calls (at teacher's preference)
- ☑ Virtual office hours (2x per week, drop-in)
- ☑ Online community forum for peer support
- ☑ Troubleshooting guides and FAQ resources
- ☑ Access to expert consultants (curriculum, technical, assessment)

### 4. Equipment Support (if applicable)
- ☑ Equipment grant of $________ (if awarded)
- ☑ Bulk purchasing assistance for equipment discounts
- ☑ Technical specifications and vendor recommendations
- ☑ Equipment maintenance and troubleshooting guidance

### 5. Evaluation and Documentation Support
- ☑ All assessment instruments and data collection tools
- ☑ Training on assessment administration
- ☑ Data analysis support and feedback
- ☑ Assistance with student consent/assent processes
- ☑ Case study development support (if participating)

### 6. Recognition and Dissemination
- ☑ Public recognition as pilot school (with permission)
- ☑ Certificate of participation for teachers
- ☑ Opportunity to present at conferences
- ☑ Inclusion in pilot program publications (with approval)
- ☑ Networking with other pilot schools and industry partners

## Part IV: Terms and Conditions

### Duration
This agreement is effective from _____________ to _____________ (pilot program year).

### Modifications
This agreement may be modified by mutual written consent of both parties.

### Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. In the event of termination:
- School retains access to curriculum materials obtained during participation
- School returns any loaned equipment
- School completes any data collection in progress (if possible)
- Both parties work collaboratively to ensure minimal disruption to students

### Circumstances Beyond Control
Both parties recognize that unforeseen circumstances (e.g., natural disasters, public health emergencies, personnel changes) may affect ability to fulfill commitments. In such cases, parties will communicate promptly and work together to adjust plans as needed.

### Dispute Resolution
In the event of disagreements, parties commit to:
1. Direct communication between lead teacher and program coordinator
2. Involvement of school administrator and program director if needed
3. Mediation by neutral third party if agreement cannot be reached
