# Success Story Format

## UVS Education Hub - Pilot Program Success Stories

**Purpose:** Success stories are short, compelling narratives (1-2 pages) that highlight pilot program impact. Used for marketing, advocacy, fundraising, and inspiring others.

**Audience:** General public, potential funders, educators, policymakers, media

**Length:** 500-800 words + 2-3 photos

## Essential Elements Checklist

**Every success story should include:**

☐ **Specific people** - Named individuals (with permission) or compelling characters
☐ **Concrete details** - Specific scenes, moments, quotes
☐ **Emotion** - Show how people felt, their reactions
☐ **Challenge** - What obstacles were overcome?
☐ **Transformation** - What changed? How?
☐ **Data/evidence** - At least 1-2 specific numbers or outcomes
☐ **Quotes** - 2-3 direct quotes from participants
☐ **Relevance** - Why should readers care?
☐ **Photos** - 2-3 high-quality, caption photos with releases

## Sample Success Story Outline Options

### Option 1: Student-Centered Story

**Focus:** Individual student transformation

**Structure:**
1. Meet the student (background, before program)
2. Their journey through the program (experiences, challenges)
3. What they learned/achieved
4. Their future aspirations
5. Broader significance

**Best for:** Demonstrating impact on individual students, inspiring others

---

### Option 2: Teacher-Centered Story

**Focus:** Teacher innovation and growth

**Structure:**
1. Teacher background (why they took this on)
2. Preparation and launch (fears, hopes)
3. Implementation journey (what they learned)
4. Student impact observed
5. Teacher's transformation and advice

**Best for:** Recruiting teachers, professional development promotion

---

### Option 3: School/Community Story

**Focus:** Program as catalyst for change

**Structure:**
1. School/community context (before)
2. Decision to implement program
3. Implementation across school/community
4. Ripple effects (students, teachers, community)
5. Sustainability and vision

**Best for:** Advocacy, demonstrating scalability, securing support

---

### Option 4: Partnership Story

**Focus:** Collaboration between school and industry/community

**Structure:**
1. Introduction of both partners
2. How partnership formed
3. What each contributed
4. Joint activities and outcomes
5. Benefits for both parties and students

**Best for:** Encouraging partnerships, demonstrating community engagement

---

## Writing Tips

### Do:
✓ Use active voice and strong verbs
✓ Show, don't just tell (use specific examples)
✓ Include sensory details (what did it look/sound/feel like?)
✓ Use short paragraphs for readability
✓ Include direct quotes from real people
✓ Use first names (with permission) for intimacy
✓ Include data to support narrative
✓ End with forward-looking, inspirational message

### Don't:
✗ Use jargon or technical language
✗ Be overly promotional or "salesy"
✗ Make unsupported claims
✗ Include only successes (mention challenges overcome)
✗ Write in passive voice
✗ Use generic stock photos
✗ Violate privacy (get all necessary releases)
✗ Forget to fact-check details

## Drafting Process

### Step 1: Gather Information
☐ Interview key people (students, teachers, administrators)
☐ Collect quotes
☐ Gather data/outcomes
☐ Review documentation (reflections, surveys, assessments)
☐ Obtain photos (with releases)

### Step 2: Choose Angle
☐ Decide on primary focus (student/teacher/school/partnership)
☐ Identify most compelling aspect of story
☐ Choose story structure

### Step 3: Draft
☐ Write first draft (don't worry about length)
☐ Include all key elements
☐ Use quotes and specific examples

### Step 4: Revise
☐ Tighten to 500-800 words
☐ Strengthen opening hook
☐ Ensure clear story arc
☐ Add subheadings for readability
☐ Fact-check all details
☐ Proofread

### Step 5: Finalize
☐ Get approval from featured individuals
☐ Confirm all releases obtained
☐ Add final photos with captions
☐ Submit to pilot program coordinator
