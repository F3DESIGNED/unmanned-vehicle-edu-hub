## Additional Information and FAQs

### Why do we need this permission?

We want to document and share the great work happening in our pilot program. Photos and videos help us:
- Show what students are learning and achieving
- Inspire other students and schools
- Advocate for program support and funding
- Improve our program through documentation
- Celebrate student success

### What if I don't want to give permission?

That's completely fine! Participation in the program is not dependent on granting photo/video permission. Students who do not have releases may:
- Still participate fully in all activities
- Be excluded from group photos (positioned outside camera view)
- Have images blurred or cropped if incidentally included
- Request that photographer/videographer avoid capturing them

### Can I limit permission?

Yes! You can:
- Allow photos but not videos
- Allow use for research but not promotion
- Allow use without names
- Specify other restrictions in Section 7

### Can I change my mind later?

Yes, you may revoke permission at any time by notifying us in writing. We will stop using new materials, but cannot retrieve materials already distributed.

### How will materials be used respectfully?

We commit to:
- Using photos/videos in appropriate educational contexts
- Not using materials in ways that could embarrass participants
- Representing students' work accurately
- Obtaining additional permission for high-profile uses
- Responding to concerns about how materials are used

### Will photos be shared on social media?

Only if you grant that specific permission. We recognize that social media posts can be widely shared beyond our control. If you have concerns about social media use, you can limit permission accordingly.
