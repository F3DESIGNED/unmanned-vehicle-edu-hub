## Phase 7: First Week of Implementation

### ✓ Launch Activities

- [ ] **First day:**
  - Welcome students and build excitement
  - Review safety rules and expectations
  - Administer pre-assessments
  - Collect consent/assent forms
  - Begin first lesson/activity

- [ ] **First week:**
  - Establish classroom routines
  - Complete safety training unit
  - Introduce course overview and learning objectives
  - Begin building classroom community
  - Conduct initial hands-on activity

### ✓ Initial Data Collection

- [ ] **Pre-assessments administered:**
  - Knowledge test completed by all students
  - Skills baseline assessed
  - Attitude survey completed
  - Data submitted to pilot program

- [ ] **Consent/assent tracking:**
  - Track consent form returns
  - Follow up with families who haven't returned forms
  - Submit consent/assent summary to pilot program

### ✓ Communication with Pilot Program

- [ ] **Week 1 check-in:**
  - Complete first weekly reflection log
  - Report: all going well, or issues needing support?
  - Share first week photos/highlights (if available)
  - Schedule first coaching call (if not already scheduled)

## Ongoing Throughout Pilot Program

### ✓ Regular Communication and Reflection

- [ ] **Weekly:**
  - Complete weekly reflection log (5-10 minutes)
  - Document challenges and successes
  - Note questions for coaching or learning community

- [ ] **Monthly:**
  - Complete monthly implementation survey
  - Attend (or watch recording of) monthly learning community meeting
  - Collect and organize student work samples

- [ ] **Each semester:**
  - Complete mid-semester and end-of-semester comprehensive reflections
  - Participate in evaluation activities (observations, interviews)
  - Administer mid-year or end-of-year assessments

### ✓ Continuous Improvement

- [ ] **After each unit:**
  - Reflect on what worked well and what to adjust
  - Update lesson plans for next time
  - Share insights with pilot community

- [ ] **Responsive support:**
  - Reach out for technical assistance when needed
  - Attend office hours when questions arise
  - Collaborate with cohort peers for problem-solving
