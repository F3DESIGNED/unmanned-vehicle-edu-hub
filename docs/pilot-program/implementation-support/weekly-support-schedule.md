# Weekly Support Schedule - Pilot Program

## Overview

The UVS Education Hub Pilot Program provides comprehensive, responsive support throughout your implementation journey. This document outlines the weekly support rhythm and available resources.

**Your support team is here to help you succeed!**

---

## Weekly Support Rhythm

### Monday: Week Planning and Resources

**What's Available:**
- **Curriculum Planning Email** (Mondays 8am ET)
  - Preview of current week's lessons
  - Key concepts and learning objectives
  - Materials needed
  - Teaching tips and common misconceptions
  - Suggested pacing

- **Resource Drop** (Mondays)
  - Supplementary materials added to resource library
  - New videos, articles, activities
  - Updated FAQs based on previous week's questions

**What You Can Do:**
- Review week's curriculum
  - Gather materials needed
- Plan adjustments based on your context
- Identify questions for office hours

---

### Tuesday: Virtual Office Hours (Technical Support)

**Time:** 3:00-4:30 PM ET (12:00-1:30 PM PT)

**Format:** Drop-in Zoom session

**Focus:** Technical troubleshooting and questions

**Who:** Technical support specialist + pilot program coordinator

**Topics We Can Help With:**
- Equipment troubleshooting (drones not flying, connectivity issues, etc.)
- Software/programming questions
- Flight controller configuration
- Safety and FAA compliance questions
- Setup and calibration issues
- Repair and maintenance

**How to Participate:**
- Join Zoom link (sent via email, also posted in online community)
- Use "raise hand" feature or wait for your turn
- Share screen if demonstrating a problem
- Can stay for entire session or just pop in with questions

**Can't Attend Live?**
- Submit questions via form: [link to technical question form]
- Response within 24 hours
- Common questions addressed in FAQ

**Zoom Link:** _________________________________

### Wednesday: Curriculum and Pedagogy Office Hours

**Time:** 4:00-5:00 PM ET (1:00-2:00 PM PT)

**Format:** Drop-in Zoom session

**Focus:** Curriculum implementation, instructional strategies, differentiation

**Who:** Curriculum developers + experienced pilot teachers (rotating)

**Topics We Can Help With:**
- Lesson planning and pacing questions
- Differentiation strategies
- Assessment and grading questions
- Student engagement challenges
- Adapting curriculum for your context
- Connecting to standards
- Managing group work and projects
- Dealing with mixed ability levels

**How to Participate:**
- Join Zoom link (sent via email)
- Come with specific questions or scenarios
- Share what you're working on
- Learn from others' questions too

**Can't Attend Live?**
- Submit questions via curriculum Q&A forum
- Watch recording (posted within 24 hours)
- Request one-on-one consultation time

**Zoom Link:** _________________________________

### Friday: Reflection and Planning

**What's Available:**
- **Weekly Reflection Prompt** (Fridays 3pm ET)
  - Emailed reflection questions for the week
  - 5-10 minute reflection template
  - Optional: Share in community forum

**What You Can Do:**
- Complete weekly reflection log (required, ~5-10 min)
- Review week's successes and challenges
- Note adjustments for next week
- Submit reflection (link in email or online form)
- Plan for next week

**Why This Matters:**
- Helps you track progress and growth
- Informs our support to you
- Contributes to program evaluation
- Builds reflective practice habits

## Monthly Support Activities

**Time:** 6:00-7:00 PM ET (3:00-4:00 PM PT)

**Participation:**

- Recording available within 24 hours

- Bring questions and insights

## Support Timeline for New Pilot Schools

### First Month: High-Touch Support

**Week 1:**
- Daily check-in email (days 1-5)
- Special "Week 1 Survival" office hours
- Troubleshooting any launch issues

**Week 2-4:**
- Regular weekly support rhythm begins
- Additional coaching calls available
- Close monitoring of reflections for red flags

### Months 2-3: Establishing Rhythm

- Weekly support continues
- Independence growing
- Proactive support as needs arise

### Months 4+: Ongoing Partnership

- Consistent weekly structure
- You know when and how to access support
- Relationship with coordinator established
- Peer network strong

## Sample Weekly Schedule for Pilot Teacher

**Monday:**
- Read curriculum planning email (5 min)
- Review week's lessons and gather materials (30-45 min)

**Tuesday:**
- Attend technical office hours IF needed (30-60 min)
- OR work ahead on lesson planning

**Wednesday:**
- Attend curriculum office hours IF needed (30-60 min)
- OR catch up on grading, student work

**Thursday:**
- Read community digest email (5 min)
- Browse forum for ideas and resources (10-15 min)

**Friday:**
- Complete weekly reflection (5-10 min)
- Plan for next week

**Monthly (first Tuesday):**
- Attend learning community meeting (60 min)

**Monthly (mid-month):**
- Coaching call (30-45 min, scheduled flexibly)

**Monthly (end of month):**
- Complete implementation survey (10-15 min)

**Total weekly time commitment for support/PD:** ~1-2 hours (varies by needs)
