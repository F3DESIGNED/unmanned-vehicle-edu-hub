## How to Maximize Office Hours

### Before Attending:
✅ Prepare specific questions or scenarios
✅ Gather relevant materials (lesson plans, student work, equipment)
✅ Test your Zoom connection
✅ Have curriculum materials accessible

### During Office Hours:
✅ Use "raise hand" feature or chat to queue up
✅ Be ready to share screen if demonstrating an issue
✅ Take notes on advice and resources shared
✅ Listen to others' questions - you might learn something!
✅ Contribute your own ideas and solutions

### After Office Hours:
✅ Implement strategies discussed
✅ Share outcomes in forum (what worked?)
✅ Follow up if you need clarification
✅ Pay it forward - help others with similar questions

## Office Hours Norms

**Respectful and Professional:**
- All participants treated with respect
- Confidentiality maintained (don't share others' specific challenges outside group)
- Create supportive, judgment-free space

**Focused and Efficient:**
- Come prepared with specific questions
- Be concise to allow time for everyone
- Follow up complex issues individually

**Collaborative:**
- Share your expertise and experiences
- Learn from each other
- Build community

**Flexibility:**
- OK to join late or leave early
- Turn camera on or off based on comfort
- Participate via chat if prefer not to speak
