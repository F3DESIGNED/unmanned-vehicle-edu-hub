## 4. Pilot School Participation

### 4.1 School Engagement

**Accessing pilot schools:**
- University researchers must apply through research partnership process
- Schools have right to decline participation
- All school-based activities require school approval
- Minimize burden on schools and teachers
- Coordinate schedules in advance

**Program facilitates but does not guarantee:**
- University researchers must build relationships with schools
- Schools make final decisions about participation
- Payment or incentives may be needed for school participation

### 4.2 School Protections

- Require modifications to protocols
- Decline participation without penalty
