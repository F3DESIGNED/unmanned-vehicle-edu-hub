# UGV Systems

Welcome to the UGV (Unmanned Ground Vehicle) Systems section, your resource for ground-based robotics in education.

## Purpose

This section covers autonomous and remote-controlled ground vehicles, from simple Arduino rovers to advanced Raspberry Pi-powered systems with computer vision and autonomous navigation.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Arduino-Based Rovers** - Simple, cost-effective ground vehicles
- **Raspberry Pi Vehicles** - Advanced computing and sensors
- **Autonomous Navigation** - GPS waypoints and obstacle avoidance
- **Sensor Integration** - Ultrasonic, LIDAR, cameras, and more
- **Competition Robots** - BattleBots, autonomous challenges

## Platforms Covered

- **Arduino** - Uno, Mega, ESP32 for basic control
- **Raspberry Pi** - Advanced computing and computer vision
- **ArduRover** - GPS-enabled autonomous ground vehicles
- **Custom Platforms** - Build your own chassis

## Advantages of UGVs for Education

- **Indoor friendly** - Can be used in classrooms
- **Lower cost** - Generally cheaper than UAVs
- **Fewer regulations** - No flight restrictions
- **Safer** - Lower risk compared to aerial systems
- **More hands-on** - Easier for students to interact with
