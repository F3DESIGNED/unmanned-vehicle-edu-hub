# Certifications for Drone Careers

## Required Certifications

### FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate
**Required for**: All commercial drone operations in US
**Cost**: $175 (exam fee) + $5 (FTN number)
**Validity**: 2 years (recurrent training required)
**Pass rate**: ~90% with proper preparation

**Study time**: 20-40 hours
**Difficulty**: Moderate

See [Part 107 Guide](./part-107-guide-outline.md) for detailed preparation plan.

## Certification Pathways

### High School Student
1. Focus on learning fundamentals first
2. Part 107 at age 16+
3. Build experience before additional certs

### College Student
1. Part 107 early (freshman/sophomore year)
2. Manufacturer certs for resume
3. Specialty certs related to career goals

### Career Changer
1. Part 107 immediately
2. Industry-specific certs based on target sector
3. Professional development courses

## ROI Analysis

**Part 107**:
- Cost: ~$200
- Time: 30 hours
- Salary impact: Enables commercial work
- **ROI**: Very high

**AUVSI TOP**:
- Cost: $500-$1,500
- Time: 40-80 hours
- Salary impact: 5-10% for operations roles
- **ROI**: Moderate to high

**Manufacturer Certs**:
- Cost: $500-$2,000 each
- Time: 20-40 hours
- Salary impact: Resume value, slight edge
- **ROI**: Moderate
