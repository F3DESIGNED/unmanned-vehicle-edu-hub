## Interview Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Research company thoroughly
- [ ] Review job description  
- [ ] Prepare project descriptions (3-5)
- [ ] Practice technical explanations
- [ ] Prepare STAR stories (5-7)
- [ ] Prepare questions to ask
- [ ] Mock interview practice
- [ ] Portfolio ready to show
- [ ] Professional attire ready
- [ ] Plan arrival logistics

## After the Interview

- Send thank you email within 24 hours
- Follow up if no response in stated timeframe
- Reflect on what went well/what to improve
- Continue other job searches (don't wait)
