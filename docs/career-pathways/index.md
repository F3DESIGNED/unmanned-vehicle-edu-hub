# Career Pathways in Unmanned Vehicle Systems

Connecting education to career opportunities in the rapidly growing unmanned systems industry.

## Industry Overview

The unmanned vehicle systems industry is experiencing explosive growth across multiple sectors:

- **Market Size**: $50+ billion globally and growing 15-20% annually
- **Job Growth**: Projected 20,000+ new jobs by 2030 in US alone
- **Salary Range**: $50K-$150K+ depending on role and experience
- **Geographic Distribution**: Opportunities nationwide, concentrated in tech hubs and agricultural regions

## Career Tracks

### Technical Paths

**Autonomy & Software** (High demand)
- Develop autonomous systems
- Machine learning and AI
- Path planning and control
- Salary: $80K-$150K+

**Hardware & Systems** (Growing demand)
- Design drone platforms
- Sensor integration
- Power systems
- Salary: $70K-$130K

**Computer Vision** (Very high demand)
- Image processing
- Object detection and tracking
- 3D reconstruction
- Salary: $85K-$150K+

**Data Analysis** (High demand)
- Process aerial imagery
- GIS and mapping
- Business intelligence
- Salary: $60K-$120K

### Operational Paths

**Pilot/Operator** (Immediate opportunity)
- Remote pilot in command
- Data collection specialist
- Emergency response operator
- Salary: $45K-$85K

**Mission Planning** (Moderate demand)
- Flight planning
- Operations coordination
- Airspace management
- Salary: $55K-$95K

**Maintenance & Support** (Growing)
- Equipment repair
- Technical support
- Calibration and testing
- Salary: $50K-$90K

### Business Paths

**Entrepreneur** (Risk/reward)
- Start drone services company
- Develop products
- Consulting services
- Income: Highly variable

**Sales & Marketing** (Steady demand)
- Technical sales
- Product demonstration
- Customer success
- Salary: $50K-$120K+ (with commissions)

**Program Management** (Experienced)
- Manage drone programs
- Coordinate projects
- Budget and resources
- Salary: $80K-$140K

## Educational Pathways

### Entry-Level Roles
**Education**: High school diploma + FAA Part 107 certification

**Typical jobs**:
- Drone operator/pilot
- Data collection technician
- Field support specialist

**How to prepare**: Complete this curriculum, earn Part 107, build portfolio

### Mid-Level Technical Roles
**Education**: Associate's degree or bachelor's in progress

**Typical jobs**:
- Junior software engineer
- Technical specialist
- Applications engineer

**How to prepare**: Continue to advanced topics, internships, projects portfolio

### Advanced Technical Roles
**Education**: Bachelor's degree in engineering, CS, or related field

**Typical jobs**:
- Autonomy engineer
- Computer vision engineer
- Robotics engineer

**How to prepare**: Bachelor's degree, advanced projects, research experience

### Research & Leadership Roles
**Education**: Master's or PhD

**Typical jobs**:
- Research scientist
- Principal engineer
- Technical leader

**How to prepare**: Graduate school, publications, industry experience

## Certifications & Credentials

### Required
- **FAA Part 107**: Remote Pilot Certificate (required for commercial operations in US)
  - See [Part 107 guide](./certifications/part-107-guide-outline.md)
  - Cost: $175 exam fee
  - Validity: 2 years

### Valuable
- **AUVSI Trusted Operator Program (TOP)**: Industry-recognized training
- **Drone manufacturer certifications**: DJI, Autel, etc.
- **Industry-specific**: Agricultural applicator licenses, etc.

### Professional Development
- **Engineering certifications**: PE license (for engineering roles)
- **Project management**: PMP, Agile certifications
- **Technical**: ROS developer, AWS certifications

See [certifications overview](./certifications/certification-overview.md) for details

## Salary & Compensation

### Entry-Level (0-2 years)
- Operators/Pilots: $40K-$60K
- Junior developers: $55K-$75K
- Technicians: $35K-$55K

### Mid-Career (3-7 years)
- Senior operators: $60K-$85K
- Software engineers: $80K-$120K
- Specialists: $70K-$100K

### Experienced (8+ years)
- Principal engineers: $120K-$180K+
- Technical leads: $110K-$160K
- Directors: $130K-$200K+

**Note**: Salaries vary significantly by location, industry sector, and company size. Tech hubs and defense contractors typically pay higher.
