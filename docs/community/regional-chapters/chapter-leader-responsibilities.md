# Chapter Leader Responsibilities

## Overview

Regional chapter leaders organize and facilitate local communities of unmanned systems educators. This is a volunteer leadership role focused on service and community building.

## Time Commitment

**Average: 3-5 hours per month**
- Meeting prep and facilitation: 2-3 hours
- Communication and coordination: 1-2 hours
- Planning and admin: 1 hour

**Shared leadership recommended** - Distribute tasks among 2-3 co-leaders

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Organize Meetings

**Frequency:** 3-4 times per year minimum (monthly recommended)

**Tasks:**
- Schedule meetings
- Secure locations (or set up virtual)
- Plan agendas
- Send invitations and reminders
- Prepare materials

### 2. Facilitate Meetings

**During meetings:**
- Welcome members and guests
- Keep discussion on track
- Ensure inclusive participation
- Manage time
- Create welcoming atmosphere

**After meetings:**
- Share notes and resources
- Follow up on action items
- Thank participants and speakers

### 3. Build and Maintain Community

**Ongoing:**
- Welcome new members
- Foster connections among members
- Encourage participation
- Create sense of belonging
- Address conflicts or issues

**Outreach:**
- Recruit new members
- Promote chapter in region
- Connect with schools and organizations
- Build chapter visibility

### 4. Communication

**Regular:**
- Maintain contact list
- Send meeting reminders (1 week and 1 day before)
- Share resources and opportunities
- Respond to member inquiries

**Channels:**
- Email list
- Group chat or forum
- Social media (optional)
- Connection to global community

### 5. Coordination

**With global community:**
- Register chapter and update info
- Share chapter activities and successes
- Access resources and support
- Participate in leader community

**Within chapter:**
- Coordinate with co-leaders
- Plan activities collaboratively
- Delegate responsibilities
- Develop future leaders

### 6. Documentation

**Basic records:**
- Meeting dates and attendance
- Key activities and outcomes
- Member contact information
- Resources shared
- Success stories

**Annual report:**
- Year in review
- Impact and outcomes
- Challenges and lessons
- Plans for next year

## Leadership Skills

**Essential:**
- Organization and planning
- Communication
- Facilitation
- Inclusivity and welcoming
- Problem-solving

**Nice to have:**
- Public speaking
- Event planning
- Conflict resolution
- Fundraising

## What Leaders Are NOT Expected To Do

❌ Know everything about unmanned systems
❌ Be the sole expert
❌ Solve everyone's problems
❌ Pay for chapter expenses personally
❌ Be available 24/7
❌ Do all the work alone

## Support for Leaders

**You have access to:**
- Leadership training and resources
- Global community support
- Network of other chapter leaders
- Templates and tools
- Liability guidance
- Promotion and visibility

**Monthly leader office hours:**
- Share challenges and solutions
- Learn from other leaders
- Get advice and support
- Build leader network

## Leadership Development

**Growth opportunities:**
- Present at global events
- Write about your experience
- Mentor new chapter leaders
- Serve on community committees
- Contribute to global initiatives

## Transitioning Out

**When you need to step down:**

1. **Give notice** (3-6 months ideal)
2. **Identify successors** from chapter members
3. **Train new leaders**
   - Share processes and contacts
   - Co-lead meetings during transition
   - Provide ongoing consultation
4. **Document** what you know
5. **Stay involved** as member if desired

## Self-Care

**Remember:**
- This is volunteer work
- Set boundaries
- Share the load
- Take breaks when needed
- Ask for help
- It's okay to step back
- Your wellbeing matters

**Warning signs of burnout:**
- Dreading chapter work
- Resentment toward members
- Declining enthusiasm
- Physical/emotional exhaustion
- Neglecting other responsibilities

**If burned out:**
- Talk to co-leaders or global support
- Redistribute responsibilities
- Take a break
- Consider transitioning out
- It's okay - you've served well!

## Recognition

**Chapter leaders receive:**
- Leadership badge
- Recognition in community
- Professional development credit
- Resume/portfolio building
- Network and visibility
- Personal growth
- Deep satisfaction!

## FAQs

**Q: What if no one comes to meetings?**
A: Survey members about barriers, try different formats/times, focus on value. Sometimes takes time to build momentum.

**Q: What if members have conflicts?**
A: Address directly, facilitate resolution, uphold community guidelines, involve global support if needed.

**Q: What if I make mistakes?**
A: Everyone does! Learn, adjust, keep going. Community is forgiving.

**Q: What if I don't know something a member asks?**
A: Perfect! Connect them to resources or experts. You're a facilitator, not oracle.

**Q: Can I step down if it's not working out?**
A: Absolutely. Give notice if possible, help with transition, no judgment.
