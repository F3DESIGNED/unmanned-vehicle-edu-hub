# Annual Conference Overview

The Unmanned Vehicle Systems Education Hub Annual Conference is our flagship event, bringing together educators, students, researchers, and industry partners for learning, networking, and celebration.

## Conference Vision

A gathering that:
- Showcases student work and educator innovation
- Provides high-quality professional development
- Builds community connections
- Celebrates achievements
- Charts the future of the field

## Conference Format

### Duration
**3 days:**
- Day 1: Workshops and pre-conference events
- Day 2: Main conference (keynotes, sessions, expo)
- Day 3: Extended sessions, competitions, networking

### Attendance
- Target: 200-500 participants
- Educators, students, researchers, industry
- Virtual and in-person options

### Location
Rotates annually among regions to ensure access

## Conference Components

### Keynote Addresses
Inspiring talks from leaders in education and technology

### Concurrent Sessions (60-90 min)
- Technical skills workshops
- Pedagogy deep-dives
- Research presentations
- Panel discussions

### Poster Sessions
Visual presentations of:
- Student projects
- Research findings

- Innovative resources

### Expo Hall
- Educational product vendors
- University programs
- Industry partners
- Community organizations

### Student Competitions
- Flying challenges
- Programming competitions
- Design competitions
- Presentation competitions

### Networking Events
- Welcome reception
- Affinity group meetups
- Regional chapter gatherings
- Awards banquet

### Professional Learning
- Earn professional development credits
- Certificate programs
- Workshop series
- Credentials

### Registration Tiers

**Early Bird** (6+ months):
- Reduced rates
- Priority session selection
- Hotel discounts

**Regular** (2-6 months):
- Standard rates
- Good session availability

**Late** (< 2 months):
