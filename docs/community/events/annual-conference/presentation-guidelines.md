# Presentation Guidelines

Congratulations on being selected to present! These guidelines will help you prepare an excellent session.

## General Guidelines

### Timing
- **Arrive 15 minutes early** to your session
- **Start and end on time** - attendees have tight schedules
- **Leave time for Q&A** (10-15 min recommended)
- **Practice timing** before conference

### Content
- **Focus on practical application** - what can attendees DO with this?
- **Use examples** from your real experience
- **Share failures** and lessons learned, not just successes
- **Make it interactive** - engage attendees
- **Provide resources** - handouts, links, templates

### Accessibility
- **Use clear, simple language**
- **Provide text alternatives** for visual content
- **Use high-contrast slides**
- **Speak clearly** into microphone
- **Describe visual demonstrations** for accessibility

## Preparing Your Presentation

### Slides

**Design:**
- Use provided template (ensures branding, accessibility)
- Font size: 24pt minimum for body text
- High contrast (dark text on light, or vice versa)
- Limit text per slide (6x6 rule: max 6 bullets, 6 words each)
- Use images and diagrams
- Include your contact info on final slide

**Content:**
- Title slide with your name, institution, contact
- Learning objectives slide
- Clear structure and flow
- Examples and visuals
- Resources and next steps
- Questions slide

**Technical:**
- Bring backup on USB drive
- Test animations and videos
- Have offline version ready
- Export as PDF backup

### Handouts

**Recommended handouts:**
- One-page summary
- Resource list with links
- Templates or worksheets
- Key takeaways
- Contact information

**Format:**
- PDF (accessible)
- Clear, readable fonts
- Organized and easy to scan
- Include conference info for reference

## Presentation Delivery

### For All Formats

**Opening (5 min):**
- Introduce yourself
- State learning objectives
- Set expectations
- Create welcoming environment

**Body (40-75 min depending on format):**
- Deliver core content
- Use examples and stories
- Check for understanding
- Maintain energy
- Manage time

**Closing (10-15 min):**
- Summarize key points
- Provide next steps
- Q&A
- Share contact info
- Thank attendees

### For Workshops

**Additional considerations:**
- Clear instructions for activities
- Check that everyone has materials
- Circulate and provide support
- Adjust pacing based on group progress
- Troubleshoot issues quickly
- Provide extension activities for fast finishers

### For Lightning Talks

**Keep it tight:**
- One main idea
- Clear structure (problem, solution, results)
- Minimal slides (5-7 max)
- Practice extensively
- End with clear call-to-action

### For Panels

**Panel guidelines:**
- Introduce all panelists
- Structure discussion (not just open conversation)
- Ensure all panelists participate
- Move to audience Q&A halfway through
- Moderator keeps time and directs traffic

### For Posters

**Poster requirements:**
- 4' x 3' maximum
- Readable from 3 feet away
- Clear title with authors
- Organized flow (top-left to bottom-right)
- Visual elements (not text-heavy)
- QR code to resources
- Be present during poster session

## Engaging Your Audience

### Interactive Strategies

**For small groups (< 30):**
- Think-pair-share
- Round-robin sharing
- Small group discussions
- Hands-on practice

**For large groups (30+):**
- Polls (show of hands or digital)
- Turn-and-talk with neighbor
- Chat participation
- Q&A throughout

**For all sizes:**
- Ask questions
- Real-world scenarios
- Problem-solving together
- Build on audience expertise

### Handling Q&A

**Good practices:**
- Repeat or rephrase questions
- Thank questioner
- Keep answers concise
- It's okay to say "I don't know"
- Offer to follow up offline
- Park detailed discussions for after

**Difficult situations:**
- Off-topic questions: "Great question for different context, let's chat after"
- Challenging questions: "Interesting perspective, what do others think?"
- No questions: Have 2-3 prepared to start discussion
