# Hosting a Webinar Guide

Congratulations on being scheduled to present a webinar! This guide will help you prepare and deliver an excellent session.

- [ ] Test everything

- [ ] Deliver great webinar!

## Content Preparation

### Structure Your Webinar

**For 60-minute webinar:**
- **Welcome (5 min):** Introduce yourself, set expectations
- **Content (40 min):** Main presentation with examples
- **Q&A (15 min):** Questions and discussion

**For 90-minute workshop:**
- **Welcome (5 min)**
- **Part 1 (25 min):** Concept introduction
- **Activity 1 (15 min):** Hands-on practice
- **Part 2 (25 min):** Advanced concepts
- **Activity 2 (15 min):** Application practice
- **Wrap-up (5 min)**

### Make It Interactive

**Engagement strategies:**
- Polls every 10-15 minutes
- Chat check-ins
- Breakout discussions (for workshops)
- Live demonstrations
- Q&A throughout, not just at end
- Hands-on practice time

### Prepare Resources

**Required resources:**
- Presentation slides (PDF)
- Any code, templates, or files
- Links to related resources
- Bibliography/references

**Nice to have:**
- Handout or one-pager
- Recording-friendly slides
- Pre-read materials
- Follow-up resources

## Technical Setup

### Equipment

**Minimum:**
- Reliable internet
- Good microphone (headset recommended)
- Webcam (preferred but optional)
- Updated Zoom client

**Recommended:**
- Backup internet (mobile hotspot)
- External microphone
- Good lighting
- Second monitor
- Glass of water nearby

### Slides

**Best practices:**
- Large, readable fonts (24pt minimum)
- High contrast
- Minimal text per slide
- Visual examples
- Your contact info on final slide

**Accessibility:**
- Add alt text to images
- Use clear language
- Provide transcript/captions
- Share slides in advance

## Presenting Tips

### Before You Start

- Join 15 min early
- Do tech check
- Welcome early participants
- Start exactly on time

### During Presentation

**Delivery:**
- Speak clearly and pace yourself
- Use examples and stories
- Show, don't just tell
- Check chat regularly
- Watch time carefully

**Engagement:**
- Address participants by name when possible
- Acknowledge chat comments
- Answer questions as they arise
- Maintain energy and enthusiasm

**Technical:**
- Mute/unmute appropriately
- Share screen cleanly
- Monitor audio quality
- Have backup plan ready

### Handling Q&A

**Good practices:**
- Repeat questions for recording
- Answer concisely
- Say "I don't know" when you don't
- Redirect to Discussions for deep dives
- Offer to follow up offline
- End on time

## Common Challenges

**Challenge: Technical difficulties**
**Solution:** Have backup ready, stay calm, use phone if needed

**Challenge: Low attendance**
**Solution:** Recording reaches more people, engage who's there

**Challenge: Difficult questions**
**Solution:** It's okay not to know, offer to research and follow up

**Challenge: Running over time**
**Solution:** Know what you can cut, end on time anyway

**Challenge: No one asking questions**
**Solution:** Have prepared questions, use polls, ask for chat feedback
