# Office Hours Hosting Guide

Thank you for volunteering to host office hours! This guide will help you facilitate effective, welcoming sessions.

## Before Your Session

**1 Week Before:**
- [ ] Confirm date/time with coordinator
- [ ] Receive Zoom link and host key
- [ ] Announcement posted to community

**2-3 Days Before:**
- [ ] Test Zoom setup
- [ ] Prepare any resources to share
- [ ] Review common questions in Discussions

**Day Of:**
- [ ] Log in 10 minutes early
- [ ] Test audio/video
- [ ] Enable waiting room
- [ ] Prepare screen shares if needed

## Session Structure (60 minutes)

### Opening (5 min)
- Welcome everyone as they join
- Brief introduction of yourself
- Explain format
- Set friendly, informal tone

### Round Robin (10-15 min)
- Each person briefly shares:
  - Name and context
  - What brings them today
  - What they hope to get help with
- Take mental notes of needs

### Open Troubleshooting (35-40 min)
- Address questions as raised
- Encourage peer-to-peer help
- Go deep when helpful
- Move on when resolved or stuck
- Share resources generously
- Direct to Discussions for follow-up

### Closing (5 min)
- Quick round: what's your next step?
- Remind about Discussions
- Announce next office hours
- Thank everyone

## Facilitation Tips

**Create welcoming space:**
- Use names
- Acknowledge everyone
- No judgment
- Normalize struggle
- Celebrate wins

**Manage time:**
- Keep track of time
- Balance depth and breadth
- Prevent domination
- Ensure everyone gets heard

**Handle "I don't know":**
- Totally fine!
- "Great question - what do others think?"
- "Let's research together"
- Point to resources or experts

**If slow:**
- Share recent Discussions
- Discuss common challenges
- Informal networking
- Preview upcoming events

**If too busy:**
- Prioritize safety issues
- Group similar questions
- Use breakout rooms
- Schedule follow-ups
- Extend time if possible

## After Your Session

**Within 24 hours:**
- [ ] Share any promised resources in Discussions
- [ ] Follow up on unresolved questions
- [ ] Brief reflection to coordinator
- [ ] Suggest improvements

## Resources for Hosts

- Community resource directory
- Common troubleshooting guides
- Recent Discussion highlights
- Contact list for specialist referrals

## Getting Help

If you need support during session:
- Use co-host if available
- Direct complex issues to Discussions
- Schedule follow-up meetings
- Connect people to mentorship program

---

**You've got this! Office hours are informal and collaborative. Your job is to facilitate, not know everything.**
