## How Office Hours Work

**Format:**
- Drop-in, no registration required
- Informal and collaborative
- Bring questions, problems, or ideas
- Help others while getting help
- Build community

**What to expect:**
- Welcome and introductions
- Round-robin sharing needs
- Collaborative troubleshooting
- Resource sharing
- Networking

**What to bring:**
- Your questions or challenges
- Willingness to help others
- Open mind and friendly attitude

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to register?**
A: No! Just show up.

**Q: What if I'm brand new?**
A: Perfect! Everyone welcome, especially beginners.

**Q: Can I just listen?**
A: Yes, though we encourage participation.

**Q: Will sessions be recorded?**
A: Generally no, to encourage open discussion. Special sessions may be recorded with advance notice.

**Q: What if I can't make these times?**
A: Contact us about adding times for different time zones or schedules.
