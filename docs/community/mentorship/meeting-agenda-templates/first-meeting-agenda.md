# First Meeting Agenda Template

**Duration**: 60 minutes
**Format**: Video call or in-person

## Pre-Meeting Preparation

**Both parties should:**
- Review each other's application highlights
- Prepare introduction talking points
- Review [Mentorship Agreement Template](../mentorship-agreement-template.md)
- Test video conferencing technology (if virtual)
- Block full hour without interruptions

## Meeting Structure

### Opening (5 minutes)

**Icebreaker:**
"Share something about yourself that's NOT related to teaching or unmanned systems!"

**Purpose:**
- Build rapport
- Ease nervousness
- Establish friendly tone

### Getting to Know Each Other (15 minutes)

**Mentee shares** (7-8 minutes):
- Teaching background and context
- Journey to unmanned systems
- Current program status
- What excites you about this field
- What keeps you up at night

**Mentor shares** (7-8 minutes):
- Your teaching journey
- How you got into unmanned systems
- Challenges you've overcome
- What you love about this work
- Your mentoring approach

**Discussion prompts:**
- What surprised you when you started?
- What do you wish you'd known earlier?
- What's been your biggest success?

## Sample First Meeting Script

### Opening

**Mentor:** "Hi [Name]! I'm so glad we're finally meeting. Before we dive into unmanned systems and teaching, tell me something interesting about yourself that has nothing to do with either of those things!"

**Mentee:** [Shares personal interest]

**Mentor:** "That's awesome! I [share connection or ask follow-up question]. For me, I love [personal interest]. Okay, now let's talk about why we're both here..."

### Transition to Goals

**Mentor:** "So I read in your application that you're hoping to launch a quadcopter program next semester. Tell me more about that vision - what does success look like for you?"

**Mentee:** [Shares vision]

**Mentor:** "I love that. Let's break that down into some concrete goals we can work toward together..."

### Establishing Expectations

**Mentor:** "I want to make sure I'm supporting you in the right ways. I'm thinking we meet once a month for about an hour, and you can email me between meetings if you hit any roadblocks. Does that work for you?"

**Mentee:** [Responds]

**Mentor:** "Great. And just to set expectations - I typically respond to emails within a day or two. If something is super urgent, like a safety concern, text me. Otherwise, email works great. Sound good?"

### Action Items

**Mentor:** "Okay, so before we meet next month, let's give you some clear things to work on. First, I think you should [suggestion]. Second, try [suggestion]. And third, take a look at [resource]. How does that feel - is that too much, too little, or just right?"

**Mentee:** [Feedback]

**Mentor:** "Perfect. And I'm going to send you [resource] and introduce you to [person] who teaches in a similar context. Deal?"

### Closing

**Mentor:** "This has been such a great first conversation. I'm really excited to work with you. Before we wrap up, tell me - on a scale of 1-10, how are you feeling about this mentorship?"

**Mentee:** [Response]

**Mentor:** "That's great to hear. I'm feeling really good about this too. One thing I want you to know - I really admire your [specific strength]. That's going to serve you so well in this work. Alright, I'll see you next month on [date]. In the meantime, don't hesitate to reach out!"

## Tips for Effective First Meeting

**For Mentors:**
- Be warm and welcoming
- Listen more than you talk
- Don't overwhelm with information
- Set realistic expectations
- End on an encouraging note
- Follow up promptly after meeting

**For Mentees:**
- Be honest about your needs
- Ask clarifying questions
- Share your concerns openly
- Don't pretend to know things you don't
- Take notes
- Express appreciation

**For Both:**
- Be fully present (no multitasking)
- Show genuine interest in each other
- Be yourself
- Focus on relationship building
- Set clear next steps
- Commit to the partnership

---

**Remember: This first meeting sets the tone for your entire mentorship. Invest in building a strong, honest, supportive relationship from day one.**
