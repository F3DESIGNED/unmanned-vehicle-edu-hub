# Quarterly Review Agenda Template

**Duration**: 90 minutes (longer than monthly check-ins)

## Pre-Meeting Preparation

**Mentee prepares:**
- Progress summary document
- Evidence of achievements (photos, student work, videos)
- Reflection on learning
- Updated goals (if needed)

**Mentor prepares:**
- Review of all previous meeting notes
- Observations on mentee's growth
- Suggestions for next quarter

## Agenda Structure

### Celebration (10 min)
- What are you most proud of this quarter?
- Share student success stories
- Review evidence of progress
- Acknowledge growth

### Deep Reflection (20 min)

**What worked well?**
- Pedagogical strategies
- Technical approaches
- Resources used
- Support systems

**What didn't work?**
- Challenges faced
- Missteps and lessons
- Resources that fell short

**What surprised you?**
- Unexpected successes
- Unforeseen challenges
- Student reactions
- Personal growth

**What have you learned about yourself as an educator?**
