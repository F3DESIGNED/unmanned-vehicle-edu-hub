# Mentee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the mentorship program! This guide helps you make the most of your mentorship experience.

## What to Expect

### Your Mentor Will:
✅ Meet with you monthly (or more frequently)
✅ Listen to your challenges and successes
✅ Ask guiding questions to help you think through problems
✅ Share relevant experiences and resources
✅ Provide accountability and encouragement
✅ Respond to async communication within 48 hours
✅ Celebrate your growth and achievements

### Your Mentor Won't:
❌ Know everything (and that's okay!)
❌ Solve all your problems for you
❌ Do the work for you
❌ Be available 24/7
❌ Make decisions for you
❌ Judge your struggles or setbacks

## Setting Yourself Up for Success

### Before Your First Meeting

**1. Clarify Your Goals**
What do you want to achieve in 6 months?
- Be specific and realistic
- Focus on 2-3 primary goals
- Think about both skills and outcomes

**2. Identify Your Needs**
What type of support would help most?
- Technical troubleshooting?
- Curriculum design?
- Classroom management?
- Confidence building?
- Resource connections?

**3. Prepare Questions**
What do you most want to ask your mentor?

**4. Gather Context**
Have ready to share:
- Your teaching situation
- Current program status
- Resources available
- Constraints you're working within

### During Meetings

**Be Present:**
- Block full hour without interruptions
- Close unnecessary tabs/apps
- Take notes
- Ask questions
- Be honest about challenges

**Be Honest:**
- About what you don't know
- When you're struggling
- If something isn't working
- About your capacity and constraints

**Be Active:**
- Don't just listen passively
- Engage in discussion
- Push back if needed
- Ask for clarification
- Share your thinking

### Between Meetings

**Follow Through:**
- Complete agreed-upon action items
- Try suggested strategies
- Document what happens
- Report back on results

**Communicate:**
- Reach out if stuck on something
- Share wins and breakthroughs
- Ask for resources when needed
- Give updates on progress

**Reflect:**
- Journal about your learning
- Note what's working
- Identify patterns
- Recognize your growth

## Making the Most of Mentorship

### Ask Good Questions

**Instead of:** "How do I teach PID control?"
**Ask:** "I want to teach PID control to 10th graders. I tried [approach] but they seemed confused. What strategies have worked for you?"

**Instead of:** "My motors won't spin."
**Ask:** "My motors won't spin. I've checked [A, B, C]. I think it might be [X] but I'm not sure how to diagnose it. What would you try next?"

### Share Context

Help your mentor help you:
- What have you already tried?
- What constraints are you working within?
- What does success look like?
- What's your timeline?
- What resources do you have access to?

### Embrace Failure

**Remember:**
- Failure is part of learning
- Your mentor has failed too
- Share what didn't work
- Reflect on lessons learned
- Try again with new insight

### Take Ownership

**This is YOUR mentorship:**
- Set the agenda based on your needs
- Speak up if something isn't working
- Adjust goals as needed
- Ask for what you need
- Take responsibility for your growth

## Common Mentee Concerns

**"I don't want to bother my mentor with 'stupid' questions."**
→ There are no stupid questions! Your mentor is here to help. Ask away.

**"I feel like I should be further along by now."**
→ Everyone's journey is different. Focus on your progress, not comparison.

**"I didn't complete my action items."**
→ That's okay! Discuss what got in the way. Adjust expectations.

**"My mentor's situation is so different from mine."**
→ Great mentorship transcends context. Focus on principles and adapt to your situation.

**"I'm not sure this is working for me."**
→ Talk to your mentor first. If needed, contact the coordinator. Re-matching is always an option.

## Red Flags

Contact program coordinator if:
- Mentor is consistently unavailable or unresponsive
- Mentor is dismissive or judgmental
- Boundaries feel inappropriate
- Communication breaks down
- You feel unsafe or uncomfortable
- Match clearly isn't working

## Measuring Your Progress

Every month, reflect:
- What did I learn?
- What did I accomplish?
- What challenges did I overcome?
- How has my confidence changed?
- What do I want to focus on next?

Every quarter, assess:
- Am I making progress toward my goals?
- Is this mentorship helping me grow?
- What adjustments should I make?
- What am I proud of?

## Graduating from the Program

After 6-12 months:
- Celebrate your growth
- Acknowledge your mentor's support
- Reflect on your journey
- Set goals for continued development
- Consider mentoring others in the future
- Stay connected to the community
