# Mentorship Matching Process

This document explains how we match mentors and mentees to create successful, productive partnerships.

## Matching Philosophy

Great mentorship relationships are built on:
- **Complementary expertise and needs**
- **Compatible communication styles**
- **Aligned values and goals**
- **Practical compatibility** (schedules, time zones)
- **Mutual respect and chemistry**

## Matching Criteria

### Primary Factors

**1. Experience Alignment**
- Mentor's expertise matches mentee's needs
- Appropriate experience gap (not too wide or narrow)
- Similar platforms and technical focus

**2. Context Similarity**
- Similar grade levels or teaching settings
- Comparable resource constraints
- Related subject areas or program goals

**3. Goal Compatibility**
- Mentee's goals align with mentor's strengths
- Realistic expectations on both sides
- Shared vision for program success

**4. Practical Logistics**
- Compatible time zones and schedules
- Aligned program duration preferences
- Matching communication preferences

### Secondary Factors

**5. Geographic Proximity** (optional)
- Enables occasional in-person meetings
- Understanding of local regulations
- Potential for school visits

**6. Personality and Style**
- Learning/teaching style compatibility
- Communication preferences
- Problem-solving approaches

**7. Diversity and Inclusion**
- Supporting underrepresented groups
- Matching specific population expertise
- Language preferences

## Matching Process Steps

### 1. Application Intake

**For Mentees:**
- Complete application form
- Identified needs and goals
- Entered into matching pool

**For Mentors:**
- Complete application form
- References checked
- Mentor training completed
- Added to available mentors

### 2. Initial Review

Program coordinator reviews:
- Completeness of applications
- Clarity of goals and expectations
- Readiness for program
- Any special considerations

### 3. Matching Analysis

Coordinator considers:
- Technical expertise needs
- Teaching context
- Availability and preferences
- Current mentor availability
- Wait list priorities

### 4. Proposed Match

Coordinator identifies potential pairs and:
- Reviews against all criteria
- Considers alternative matches
- Selects best fit
- Prepares introduction materials

### 5. Introduction

Both parties receive:
- Partner's application highlights
- Suggested first meeting agenda
- Contact information
- Program resources

### 6. First Contact

Matched pair:
- Exchange initial emails
- Schedule first meeting
- Complete pre-meeting preparation

### 7. First Meeting

Using [First Meeting Agenda](meeting-agenda-templates/first-meeting-agenda.md):
- Get to know each other
- Clarify expectations
- Establish working relationship
- Sign mentorship agreement

### 8. Check-in

After first meeting:
- Coordinator checks in with both parties
- Ensures good fit
- Addresses any concerns
- Supports adjustments if needed

## Matching Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Perfect Match Available

**Situation:** Mentee's needs clearly align with available mentor's expertise

**Process:**
- Immediate matching
- Introduction within days
- Standard onboarding

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks from application to first meeting

### Scenario 2: Multiple Possible Matches

**Situation:** Several mentors could work well with mentee

**Process:**
- Coordinator evaluates all options
- Prioritizes based on:
  - Closest expertise alignment
  - Mentor capacity and preference
  - Logistical compatibility
  - Wait time for each mentor
- Makes best match decision

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks

### Scenario 3: Wait List

**Situation:** No ideal mentor currently available

**Process:**
- Mentee added to wait list
- Receives interim support:
  - Learning circle placement
  - Office hours connection
  - Resource recommendations
- Matched when appropriate mentor available

**Timeline:** Varies (2 weeks to 3 months)

**Communication:** Monthly updates on status

### Scenario 4: Specialized Needs

**Situation:** Mentee has very specific needs or context

**Process:**
- Broader mentor recruitment
- Consider mentor team approach
- Connect with relevant experts
- May take longer to find right match

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks

### Scenario 5: Re-matching

**Situation:** Initial match isn't working well

**Process:**
- Open communication with coordinator
- No-fault re-matching
- New match based on refined understanding
- Support through transition

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for new match

## Special Matching Considerations

### High-Demand Mentors

Some mentors are in high demand due to:
- Rare expertise
- Exceptional track record
- Specialized experience

**Approach:**
- May work with 2-3 mentees
- May use group mentoring format
- May have longer wait times

### Underrepresented Groups

We prioritize:
- Matching mentors from similar backgrounds when requested
- Supporting first-generation educators
- Connecting rural educators with rural experience
- Language matching when needed

### Time Zone Challenges

For significant time differences:
- Emphasize async communication
- Flexible meeting times
- Recording meetings for reference
- Extended response windows

### Multiple Mentee Requests

Highly motivated mentees sometimes request:
- Primary mentor for general guidance
- Secondary mentor for specific expertise
- Peer mentor for mutual support

**Considered case-by-case** based on:
- Mentee capacity
- Mentor availability
- Clear role delineation
- Coordinator approval

## FAQs

**Q: How long will I wait for a match?**
A: Most mentees are matched within 2-4 weeks. Specialized needs may take longer.

**Q: Can I request a specific person as my mentor?**
A: Yes! If you know someone in the mentor pool, we can arrange that if they're available.

**Q: What if my match isn't working?**
A: Communicate openly with your mentor first. If issues persist, contact the coordinator. Re-matching is available with no stigma.

**Q: Can I switch mentors mid-program?**
A: Yes, though we encourage working through minor issues first. Re-matching is fully supported when needed.

**Q: What if I'm not matched right away?**
A: You'll receive interim support through learning circles and office hours while we find your ideal mentor.

**Q: Can I preview my potential mentor's profile?**
A: Yes, you'll receive relevant information about your matched mentor before committing.

**Q: What if my mentor and I have different teaching philosophies?**
A: Some difference is healthy! However, fundamental misalignment is reason for re-matching.
