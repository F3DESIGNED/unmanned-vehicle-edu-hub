## Purpose

This agreement establishes expectations and commitments for our mentorship relationship, ensuring a productive, respectful, and mutually beneficial partnership.

## Mentee Commitments

I, _________________________ (mentee), commit to:

✅ Attend scheduled meetings or notify mentor 24+ hours in advance if I must reschedule
✅ Come prepared with updates, questions, and reflection
✅ Follow through on action items to the best of my ability
✅ Communicate proactively about challenges and barriers
✅ Be honest about my needs, capacity, and progress
✅ Maintain open and respectful communication
✅ Document my learning and growth
✅ Take ownership of my professional development
✅ Participate in program evaluation
✅ Give back to the community when ready

## Mentor Commitments

I, _________________________ (mentor), commit to:

✅ Attend scheduled meetings or notify mentee 24+ hours in advance if I must reschedule
✅ Listen actively and without judgment
✅ Ask guiding questions rather than always providing answers
✅ Share relevant experiences, resources, and connections
✅ Respond to async communication within 48 hours (weekdays)
✅ Maintain confidentiality about our discussions
✅ Provide honest, constructive feedback
✅ Celebrate mentee's successes and support through challenges
✅ Maintain professional boundaries
✅ Notify coordinator if I need to withdraw from program

## Mutual Commitments

We both commit to:

✅ Respect each other's time and boundaries
✅ Communicate openly, honestly, and respectfully
✅ Uphold community guidelines and values
✅ Focus on mentee's growth and development
✅ Address concerns directly before involving coordinator
✅ Maintain confidentiality (except for safety concerns)
✅ Work together to make this mentorship successful
✅ Evaluate and adjust as needed
