# Mentor Application

Thank you for your interest in becoming a mentor! Your experience and willingness to support others will make a significant impact on the community.

## Before You Apply

Please review:
- [Program Overview](program-overview.md) - Understand program structure
- [Mentor Training Guide](mentor-training-guide.md) - Preview responsibilities
- [Meeting Agenda Templates](meeting-agenda-templates/) - See what meetings involve

**Time commitment**: 2-4 hours per month for 6-12 months

## Mentor Qualifications

✅ **Required:**
- 2+ years teaching unmanned vehicle systems
- Successfully implemented curriculum with positive outcomes
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Commitment to inclusive, accessible education
- Alignment with community values and guidelines
- Ability to commit to monthly meetings

✅ **Preferred:**
- Experience with multiple platforms (UAV, UGV, or both)
- Diverse teaching contexts (different grade levels, settings)
- Troubleshooting expertise
- Curriculum development experience
- Previous mentoring or coaching experience

## Submission

**Submit your application to:**
- Email: mentorship@[placeholder]
- Or: [GitHub Discussion - Mentorship Applications](https://github.com/F3DESIGNED/unmanned-vehicle-edu-hub/discussions)

**What happens next:**

1. **Application review** (1-2 weeks)
   - Program coordinator reviews application
   - References contacted

2. **Informal interview** (30 minutes)
   - Get to know you conversation
   - Answer questions about program
   - Discuss your goals and preferences

3. **Mentor training** (2 hours)
   - Complete required training
   - Connect with other mentors

4. **Matching** (varies - could be immediate or several months)
   - Matched based on mentee needs and your expertise
   - Introduced to your mentee(s)

5. **Begin mentoring!**
   - Schedule first meeting
   - Start your mentorship journey
