# Finding Your Role in the Community

The Unmanned Vehicle Systems Education Hub community is diverse, with members at different stages of their journey. This guide helps you identify where you fit and how you can make the most impact.

## Community Roles

### 🌱 Learner

**You are a Learner if:**
- You're new to unmanned vehicle systems
- You're exploring whether this is right for your classroom
- You're building foundational knowledge
- You're completing your first projects

**Your contributions:**
- Ask thoughtful questions
- Share what you're learning
- Test and provide feedback on resources
- Document your journey
- Encourage other beginners

**Your pathway:**
1. Complete [Getting Started Guide](../../getting-started/)
2. Try a [Virtual Lab](../../interactive/virtual-labs/)
3. Join a [Learning Circle](../learning-circles/what-are-learning-circles.md)
4. Find a [Mentor](../mentorship/mentee-application.md)
5. Build your first project
6. Share your experience

**Resources for you:**
- [Welcome New Members](welcome-new-members.md)
- [Virtual Labs](../../interactive/virtual-labs/)
- [Basic Troubleshooting](../../troubleshooting/)

## Multiple Roles

Most people play multiple roles! You might be:

- Implementer + Learner (common for first year)
- Technical Expert + Mentor
- Curriculum Developer + Content Creator
- Student + Community Builder
- Implementer + Researcher

Embrace all your roles and contribute in diverse ways.

## Role Transitions

Your role will evolve:

**Year 1**: Learner → Implementer
**Year 2**: Implementer → Technical Expert or Curriculum Developer
**Year 3+**: Mentor + Community Builder + Specialized role

This is natural and expected!

## Time Commitment by Role

**Low commitment** (< 2 hours/month):
- Learner
- Occasional contributor

**Medium commitment** (2-5 hours/month):
- Active Implementer
- Regular contributor
- Learning circle participant

**High commitment** (5-10 hours/month):
- Mentor
- Content Creator
- Learning circle facilitator
- Regional chapter co-leader

**Very high commitment** (10+ hours/month):
- Curriculum Developer (major projects)
- Community Builder (chapter leader)
- Multiple active roles
- Core contributor/maintainer

## Finding Your Fit

Still unsure? Try this:

### Assessment Questions

**What energizes you?**
- Solving technical problems → Technical Expert
- Teaching and explaining → Curriculum Developer or Content Creator
- Building relationships → Mentor or Community Builder
- Hands-on building → Implementer or Technical Expert
- Helping others → Mentor or Community Builder
- Creating things → Content Creator or Curriculum Developer

**What's your experience level?**
- Beginner → Learner
- Some experience → Implementer
- Significant experience → Technical Expert, Curriculum Developer, or Mentor
- Expert → Advanced roles + Mentorship

**What's your time availability?**
- 1-2 hours/month → Learner, occasional contributor
- 3-5 hours/month → Implementer, regular contributor
- 5-10 hours/month → Mentor, facilitator
- 10+ hours/month → Major contributor, community leader

**What are your goals?**
- Learn the basics → Learner
- Implement in classroom → Implementer
- Deepen technical skills → Technical Expert
- Develop curriculum → Curriculum Developer
- Support others → Mentor
- Build community → Community Builder
- Share widely → Content Creator

### Trial Period

Not sure? Try each role for a month:

- **Month 1**: Learner - Focus on learning and asking questions
- **Month 2**: Implementer - Try resources with students
- **Month 3**: Contributor - Share something you created
- **Month 4**: Community participant - Attend events, join circles

See what feels right!

## Getting Started in Your Role

Once you've identified your role(s):

1. **Connect**: Find others in similar roles
2. **Learn**: Review resources specific to your role
3. **Contribute**: Start with small contributions
4. **Grow**: Take on more responsibility over time
5. **Reflect**: Regularly assess if your role still fits
