# Community Guidelines

## Our Commitment

The Unmanned Vehicle Systems Education Hub is dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

These guidelines apply to all community spaces, including GitHub Discussions, office hours, webinars, conferences, and any other community forums.

## Core Values

### 🤝 Respect & Inclusivity

**We value:**
- Every member's unique perspective and experience
- Diverse teaching approaches and learning styles
- Questions at all levels of complexity
- Cultural and linguistic diversity

**We do not tolerate:**
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Dismissing others' experiences or concerns

### 📚 Learning-Focused

**We encourage:**
- Asking questions without fear of judgment
- Sharing both successes and failures
- Constructive feedback and peer review
- Collaborative problem-solving

**We discourage:**
- Gatekeeping or elitism ("you should already know this")
- Dismissing beginner questions
- Showing off without pedagogical value
- Competing rather than collaborating

### 🌟 Generosity & Attribution

**We expect:**
- Sharing knowledge and resources freely
- Giving proper credit for others' work
- Acknowledging inspiration and sources
- Contributing to collective knowledge

**We do not accept:**
- Plagiarism or uncredited copying
- Claiming others' work as your own
- Hoarding information for personal advantage
- Violating licenses or intellectual property

### 🔒 Safety First

**We prioritize:**
- Student safety in all activities
- Responsible sharing of safety information
- Compliance with regulations and best practices
- Ethical use of unmanned vehicle technology

**We will not allow:**
- Encouraging unsafe practices
- Circumventing safety features
- Sharing information about illegal activities
- Putting students or others at risk

## Expected Behavior

### Do:

✅ **Be welcoming and patient**
- Remember everyone was a beginner once
- Provide context when answering questions
- Offer encouragement and support

✅ **Communicate respectfully**
- Use inclusive language
- Assume good intentions
- Disagree without being disagreeable
- Provide constructive criticism

✅ **Give and receive feedback gracefully**
- Frame feedback as suggestions, not demands
- Focus on the content, not the person
- Accept feedback without defensiveness
- Thank others for their input

✅ **Respect boundaries**
- Honor people's time and availability
- Don't demand immediate responses
- Respect "no" without pushing
- Keep discussion on-topic

✅ **Share responsibly**
- Verify information before sharing
- Cite sources appropriately
- Respect licenses and copyrights
- Consider student privacy (no names/faces without permission)

### Don't:

❌ **Be dismissive or condescending**
- "This is so basic..."
- "Just Google it..."
- "Everyone knows that..."
- "Why don't you already know this?"

❌ **Make it personal**
- Attacking character instead of addressing ideas
- Bringing up past conflicts
- Making assumptions about motives
- Demanding personal information

❌ **Disrupt or derail**
- Off-topic tangents
- Repeatedly pushing rejected ideas
- Spamming or cross-posting excessively
- Hijacking threads for personal agenda

❌ **Violate trust**
- Sharing private conversations publicly
- Doxxing or revealing personal information
- Breaking confidentiality
- Using information maliciously

## GitHub Discussions Guidelines

### Asking Questions

**Good question format:**
- Clear, descriptive title
- Relevant background and context
- What you've already tried
- Specific details (error messages, configurations, etc.)
- Photos/screenshots if relevant

**Example:**
```
Title: "Motor 3 not spinning on BetaFlight F4 - tried ESC calibration"

Context: I'm building my first 250mm quadcopter for a high school class.
I've followed the build guide and configured BetaFlight, but motor 3
won't spin in the motor test.

What I've tried:
- Verified motor works by connecting directly to battery
- Tried ESC calibration procedure
- Checked all wire connections
- Swapped motor to different ESC output (problem follows the ESC port)

Configuration:
- BetaFlight 4.3
- Firmware: BTFL_MATEKF405 4.3.0
- ESC: 4-in-1 30A BlHeli_S

Photos: [attached]

Any ideas what to check next?
```

### Answering Questions

**Be helpful:**
- Read the question fully before responding
- Ask clarifying questions if needed
- Explain your reasoning
- Link to relevant documentation
- Offer to follow up if needed

**Avoid:**
- RTFM responses without guidance
- Assuming questioner's knowledge level
- Over-complicating simple issues
- Providing untested speculation

### Sharing Projects & Resources

**Include:**
- Clear description and purpose
- Age/grade level (if applicable)
- Required materials/prerequisites
- Learning objectives
- Licensing information
- Attribution for borrowed elements

**Best practices:**
- Use descriptive titles
- Add relevant tags/categories
- Provide enough detail for others to replicate
- Share both successes and challenges
- Welcome feedback and questions

## Content Guidelines

### Appropriate Content

✅ Educational resources and lesson plans
✅ Technical questions and troubleshooting
✅ Student project showcases (with permission)
✅ Research and pedagogical discussions
✅ Community events and opportunities
✅ Relevant news and updates

### Inappropriate Content

❌ Off-topic discussions
❌ Commercial advertising (unless approved community partnership)
❌ Political or religious proselytizing
❌ Personal attacks or harassment
❌ Spam or excessive self-promotion
❌ Illegal activities or dangerous content
❌ Personal information without consent

### Gray Areas

Some content requires judgment:

**Promotional content**: Occasional sharing of relevant products/services is okay if it adds value to the community. Excessive promotion or affiliate marketing is not.

**Controversial topics**: If relevant to education (e.g., privacy concerns, regulation), discuss respectfully and focus on educational implications.

**Student work**: Always get appropriate permissions before sharing student names, photos, or work. Consider privacy implications.

**Criticism**: Constructive feedback on resources is welcome. Personal attacks on creators are not.

## Moderation

### Self-Moderation

We expect community members to:
- Self-moderate their own behavior
- Gently redirect off-topic discussions
- Model positive community behavior
- Support others in learning community norms

### Community Moderation

If you observe behavior that violates these guidelines:

1. **Don't engage** - Avoid escalating the situation
2. **Document** - Take screenshots if needed
3. **Report** - Use GitHub's report feature or tag @moderators
4. **Move on** - Let moderators handle the situation

### Moderator Actions

Depending on severity, moderators may:

1. **Gentle reminder** - Point to relevant guidelines
2. **Private warning** - Direct conversation with the member
3. **Temporary restriction** - Cooling-off period
4. **Permanent ban** - For severe or repeated violations

**We aim for education over punishment**, but safety and respect are non-negotiable.

### Appeal Process

If you believe moderation action was unfair:

1. Contact moderators privately
2. Explain your perspective calmly
3. Propose resolution
4. Accept final decision gracefully

## Conflict Resolution

Disagreements happen. When they do:

1. **Assume good intent** - Most conflicts are misunderstandings
2. **Communicate directly** - Address the person respectfully
3. **Focus on issues** - Not personalities
4. **Seek to understand** - Ask questions before judging
5. **Agree to disagree** - It's okay to have different approaches
6. **Involve moderators** - If direct communication doesn't resolve it

## Inclusivity Practices

### Language

- Use gender-neutral language when possible (they/them as singular)
- Avoid ableist language ("that's crazy", "blind to the issue")
- Consider global audience (not everyone celebrates the same holidays)
- Explain acronyms and jargon

### Accessibility

- Add alt text to images
- Use clear, simple language
- Provide text alternatives to videos (captions/transcripts)
- Consider colorblind-friendly palettes

### Cultural Sensitivity

- Respect different educational systems and approaches
- Acknowledge different resource constraints
- Be aware of different regulatory environments
- Value diverse perspectives

## Recognition & Appreciation

We encourage:
- Thanking helpful community members
- Upvoting useful responses
- Nominating contributors for recognition
- Celebrating milestones and achievements

Avoid:
- Competing for "most helpful" status
- Dismissing others' contributions
- Gatekeeping recognition

## Special Considerations

### Students in the Community

If you're a student:
- You're welcome here!
- Follow all the same guidelines
- Be honest about your student status
- Get teacher/parent permission for participation
- Protect your privacy (don't share personal information)

If you're an educator:
- Help students participate safely
- Monitor student engagement
- Teach responsible online behavior
- Respect student privacy

### Industry Members

Industry participation is welcome when:
- Providing genuine educational value
- Sharing expertise generously
- Respecting the non-commercial focus
- Supporting educators without expectation

Not welcome:
- Heavy sales pitches
- Treating community as lead generation
- Offering "exclusive deals" requiring personal info
- Promoting products without pedagogical context

## Updates to Guidelines

These guidelines evolve with our community. Updates are:
- Discussed with community input
- Announced clearly before implementation
- Applied consistently and fairly

## Questions About Guidelines

Unsure if something is appropriate? Ask!

- Post in [Meta Discussions](https://github.com/F3DESIGNED/unmanned-vehicle-edu-hub/discussions)
- Tag @moderators
- Attend office hours to discuss

**When in doubt, err on the side of kindness and respect.**

- Is this respectful and constructive?
- Does this add value to the community?
- Have I given proper credit?
- Is this safe and appropriate?
