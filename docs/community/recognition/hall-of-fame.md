## Selection Criteria

### Exceptional Contribution
Significant, measurable impact on:
- Community growth
- Resource development
- Educator support
- Student outcomes
- Field advancement

### Sustained Excellence
- 2+ years of active contribution
- Consistent high-quality work
- Reliable community presence
- Growing impact over time

### Leadership
- Inspires and enables others
- Builds capacity in community
- Develops new initiatives
- Mentors and guides

### Values Alignment
- Embodies community values
- Promotes accessibility and inclusion
- Shares generously
- Builds others up

## Nomination Process

**Who can nominate:**
Anyone in the community

**When:**
Nominations open annually (January - March)

**Process:**
1. Submit detailed nomination
2. Community testimonials gathered
3. Selection committee reviews
4. Inductees announced
5. Recognition at annual conference

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I nominate myself?**
A: No, nominations must come from others. Your community will recognize you when ready.

**Q: Is there a limit to inductees per year?**
A: Generally 3-5 per year to maintain significance.

**Q: Can organizations be inducted?**
A: Primarily recognizes individuals, but organizational partnerships may be recognized separately.

**Q: What if someone has made big recent contributions but hasn't been around 2 years?**
A: Other recognition paths exist (awards, spotlights). Hall of Fame requires sustained contribution.
