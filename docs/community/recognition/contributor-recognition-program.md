# Contributor Recognition Program

We celebrate and recognize community members who contribute to our mission of accessible, high-quality unmanned vehicle systems education.

## Recognition Pillars

**We recognize:**
- Sharing knowledge and resources
- Supporting fellow educators
- Creating quality content
- Building community
- Innovation and creativity
- Sustained contribution

## Recognition Levels

### Contributor 🌱
**Criteria:** Made 1+ meaningful contributions
**Recognition:** Contributor badge, profile listing

### Active Contributor 🌿
**Criteria:** Regular participation over 3+ months
**Recognition:** Enhanced badge, monthly newsletter feature

### Core Contributor 🌳
**Criteria:** Sustained, significant contributions over 6+ months
**Recognition:** Core contributor status, special acknowledgment, priority access

### Champion 🏆
**Criteria:** Exceptional, transformative contributions
**Recognition:** Annual award, conference keynote opportunity, permanent hall of fame

## Recognition Philosophy

**We believe:**
- All contributions matter
- Recognition motivates growth
- Celebrating success builds culture
- Diverse contributions valued equally
- Recognition is abundant, not scarce

**We don't:**
- Create unhealthy competition
- Recognize only "big" contributions
- Forget quiet contributors
- Make recognition a popularity contest
