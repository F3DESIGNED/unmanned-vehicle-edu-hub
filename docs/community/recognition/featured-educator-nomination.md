## What Happens Next

1. **Review** - Nominations reviewed monthly
2. **Selection** - Featured educator chosen
3. **Contact** - We reach out to educator
4. **Feature** - Story published in newsletter and discussions
5. **Recognition** - Educator receives featured badge

## Featured Educator Benefits

- Community-wide recognition
- Profile spotlight
- Interview/story feature
- Q&A opportunity
- Resource sharing platform
- Professional portfolio piece
