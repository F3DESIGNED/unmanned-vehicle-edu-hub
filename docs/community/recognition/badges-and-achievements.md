# Badges and Achievements

Digital badges recognize your contributions and growth in the community.

## How Badges Work

**Earning badges:**
- Automatic (based on GitHub activity)
- Manual (submitted for review)
- Nominated (by community members)

**Displaying badges:**
- On your GitHub profile
- In community directory
- On your personal site (exportable)

## Badge Levels

Many badges have levels:

**Bronze 🥉** - Getting started
**Silver 🥈** - Experienced
**Gold 🥇** - Advanced/Expert
**Platinum 💎** - Exceptional

## Claiming Your Badges

**Automatic badges:**
- No action needed
- Appear on profile when earned

**Self-reported badges:**
1. Submit evidence in Discussions
2. Tag @badge-coordinator
3. Review (1-2 weeks)
4. Badge awarded

**Nominated badges:**
1. Someone nominates you
2. Review by committee
3. Badge awarded if approved

## Badge Metadata

All badges include:
- Date earned
- Brief description
- Evidence link (if applicable)
- Level (if tiered)

## Displaying Your Badges

**On GitHub:**
- Automatically on profile

**External:**

## Badge FAQ

**Q: Are badges required?**
A: No! Participate as you wish. Badges are recognition, not requirements.

**Q: Can I appeal a badge decision?**
A: Yes, contact badge-coordinator@[placeholder]

**Q: How long does review take?**
A: 1-2 weeks for manually reviewed badges

**Q: Can badges be revoked?**
A: Only for code of conduct violations

**Q: Do badges expire?**
A: No, once earned, always yours
