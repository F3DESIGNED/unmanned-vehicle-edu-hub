# Book Club Format

Deep reading and discussion of educational resources.

## Overview
**Duration:** 4-10 weeks (depending on book length)
**Group Size:** 5-8 educators
**Best for:** Pedagogical learning, theoretical grounding

## Structure

### Week 1: Introduction
- Introduce book and authors
- Discuss reading schedule
- Set expectations
- Assign first reading

### Weeks 2-N: Discussion Sessions
- Discuss assigned chapters/sections
- Connect to practice
- Raise questions
- Share insights

### Final Week: Synthesis
- Reflect on entire book
- Identify takeaways
- Plan implementation
- Celebrate completion

## Meeting Agenda Template (60 min)

**Opening (5 min):**
- Check-in
- Review reading section

**Discussion Rounds (45 min):**

**Round 1: Initial Reactions (10 min)**
- What stood out to you?
- What surprised you?
- What confused you?

**Round 2: Deep Dive (20 min)**
- Discuss key concepts
- Examine specific passages
- Ask clarifying questions
- Build shared understanding

**Round 3: Application (15 min)**
- How does this apply to your teaching?
- What will you try in your classroom?
- How does this challenge current practice?
- What questions remain?

**Closing (10 min):**
- Key takeaways (each person shares one)
- Confirm next reading
- Application commitments

## Discussion Roles (Rotate weekly)

**Facilitator:** Guides discussion, manages time
**Connector:** Relates to other resources and ideas
**Questioner:** Asks probing questions
**Illustrator:** Shares examples from practice
**Note-taker:** Captures key points

## Reading Recommendations

**Pedagogical:**
- *Make It Stick* by Brown et al.
- *Understanding by Design* by Wiggins & McTighe
- STEM education research articles

**Technical:**
- Drone/robotics technical manuals
- Research papers on autonomous systems

**Interdisciplinary:**
- Ethics and technology
- History of flight
- Systems thinking

## Tips for Success
- Read ahead of schedule
- Annotate as you read
- Bring specific questions
- Connect to your practice
- Stay open to new ideas
- Don't worry about "getting it all"
