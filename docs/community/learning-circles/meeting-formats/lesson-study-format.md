# Lesson Study Format

Collaborative lesson planning, observation, and refinement.

## Overview
**Duration:** 6-8 weeks
**Group Size:** 4-6 educators
**Best for:** Curriculum development, improving teaching practice

## Structure

### Week 1: Select and Plan
- Choose lesson topic together
- Research and study content
- Draft initial lesson plan
- Assign lesson teach (volunteer)

### Week 2-3: Refine Plan
- Review draft collaboratively
- Anticipate student thinking
- Identify observation focuses
- Finalize lesson plan
- Prepare materials

### Week 4: Teach and Observe
- One member teaches lesson
- Others observe (in-person or video)
- Take detailed observation notes
- No interruption during lesson

### Week 5: Debrief
- Teacher reflects first
- Observers share what they saw (not evaluate)
- Discuss student learning
- Identify revision opportunities
- Build revised lesson plan

### Week 6: Re-Teach (Optional)
- Another member teaches revised version
- Observe changes and impacts
- Compare versions

### Week 7-8: Finalize and Share
- Document final lesson
- Create teaching notes
- Share publicly
- Reflect on process

## Meeting Agenda Template

**Opening (10 min):**
- Check-in
- Review current phase
- Clarify today's goal

**Core Work (50 min):**
- *(Varies by phase - planning, observation, debrief)*

**Next Steps (10 min):**
- Action items
- Prepare for next phase
- Schedule observation if applicable

**Closing (5 min):**
- Reflections
- Appreciations
