# Circle Facilitator Guide

## Your Role as Facilitator

**You are a guide, not a teacher:**
- Facilitate process, not content
- Ask questions more than answer
- Support peer learning
- Create safe, inclusive space
- Keep group on track
- Model collaborative learning

## Essential Facilitation Skills

### Creating Safe Space
- Welcome all questions
- Normalize struggle
- Celebrate mistakes as learning
- No judgment or criticism
- Enforce community guidelines
- Build trust gradually

### Managing Participation
- Ensure everyone contributes
- Prevent domination
- Draw out quiet members
- Use structured turn-taking
- Break into pairs when needed
- Honor different participation styles

### Time Management
- Start and end on time
- Use agenda time boxes
- Give time warnings
- Know what can be cut
- Park off-topic items
- Respect busy schedules

### Asking Powerful Questions
- "What have you tried?"
- "What do others think?"
- "How might this work in your context?"
- "What questions does this raise?"
- "What will you do next?"

### Handling Challenges
- Address issues quickly
- Private conversations when needed
- Stay neutral in disagreements
- Redirect off-topic discussions
- Manage conflict constructively
- Know when to escalate

## Meeting Facilitation Flow

### Before Meeting (48 hours prior)
- [ ] Review planned agenda
- [ ] Prepare materials
- [ ] Send reminder email with prep work
- [ ] Check technology
- [ ] Prepare breakout activities
- [ ] Queue up any links or resources

### Opening (First 10 minutes)
1. **Welcome** - Warm greeting, acknowledge everyone
2. **Check-in** - Quick round: How are you? Any wins this week?
3. **Agenda review** - Preview what we'll cover
4. **Logistics** - Timing, breaks, parking lot

### During Meeting
- **Monitor time** - Keep on track
- **Watch participation** - Ensure inclusion
- **Read the room** - Adjust as needed
- **Capture key points** - Notes or ask volunteer
- **Manage energy** - Break up long sections
- **Stay focused** - Gently redirect tangents

### Closing (Last 10 minutes)
1. **Action items** - What will you do before next time?
2. **Next session preview** - What to prepare
3. **Appreciations** - Acknowledge contributions
4. **Logistics** - Confirm next meeting
5. **Evaluation** - Quick pulse check

### After Meeting (Within 24 hours)
- [ ] Share notes and recording (if applicable)
- [ ] Follow up on questions or issues
- [ ] Send resources mentioned
- [ ] Note any concerns
- [ ] Prepare for next session

## Dealing with Common Challenges

### Challenge: Low Attendance
**Strategies:**
- Clear expectations from start
- Flexible scheduling
- Recording sessions
- Check in with absent members
- Address barriers
- May need to reschedule or condense

### Challenge: One Person Dominates
**Strategies:**
- Structured turn-taking
- "Let's hear from someone we haven't heard from yet"
- Time limits per speaker
- Small group breakouts
- Private conversation if persistent

### Challenge: No One Talks
**Strategies:**
- Start with pair discussions
- Use chat for ideas first
- Structured prompts
- Wait time after questions
- Share your own uncertainty
- Make participation easy

### Challenge: Off-Topic Discussions
**Strategies:**
- "Great point - let's add that to parking lot"
- "That's important, but let's stay focused on..."
- Acknowledge value but redirect
- Dedicate time for open discussion
- Be kind but firm

### Challenge: Technical Difficulties
**Strategies:**
- Have backup platform
- Start 5 min early for troubleshooting
- Have phone numbers
- Record for those who can't connect
- Use chat when audio fails
- Stay calm and patient

### Challenge: Conflict or Disagreement
**Strategies:**
- Acknowledge different perspectives
- Ask for evidence or reasoning
- "What might we learn from both approaches?"
- Prevent personal attacks
- Emphasize shared goals
- Take offline if heated

### Challenge: Loss of Momentum
**Strategies:**
- Revisit goals and outcomes
- Celebrate progress
- Share success stories
- Invite guest expert
- Try new format
- Get participant input

### Challenge: You Don't Know Answer
**Perfect! Say:**
- "Great question! What do others think?"
- "Let's research that together"
- "I don't know, but let's find out"
- "Who might we ask about this?"
Model that not knowing is okay!

## Facilitation Best Practices

### Planning
✅ Over-plan but stay flexible
✅ Have backup activities
✅ Know your time buffers
✅ Prep materials in advance
✅ Test technology
✅ Review previous sessions

### During Sessions
✅ Be fully present
✅ Model active listening
✅ Stay neutral
✅ Manage your own air time
✅ Be comfortable with silence
✅ Watch body language (in-person) or video cues

### Inclusion
✅ Learn names quickly
✅ Acknowledge all contributions
✅ Notice who hasn't spoken
✅ Adapt to different styles
✅ Address microaggressions
✅ Create multiple ways to participate

### Flexibility
✅ Read the room
✅ Adjust on the fly
✅ Follow emergent needs
✅ Let go of perfect plan
✅ Trust the process
✅ Embrace "good enough"

## Self-Care for Facilitators

**You can't pour from empty cup:**
- Set boundaries
- Don't over-prepare
- Share facilitation
- Take breaks when needed
- Connect with other facilitators
- Celebrate your own growth
- It's okay to make mistakes!
