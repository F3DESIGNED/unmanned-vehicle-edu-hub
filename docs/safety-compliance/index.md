# Safety and Compliance

!!! danger "Critical Information"
    **This section contains essential safety information that could prevent injury, property damage, or legal issues. Read thoroughly before operating any unmanned vehicle.**

Safety is not optional—it's fundamental to responsible operation of unmanned systems. This section covers all safety and regulatory requirements.

## Overview

Operating unmanned vehicles involves risks that must be managed through:

- **Personal safety procedures** - Protecting yourself and others
- **Equipment safety** - Proper handling of batteries, electronics, and mechanical systems
- **Regulatory compliance** - Following local, state, and federal laws
- **Institutional policies** - School and organizational requirements
- **Emergency preparedness** - Knowing how to respond to incidents

## Universal Safety Rules

These apply to ALL unmanned vehicles across ALL domains:

### Before Every Operation

- [ ] **Weather check** - Appropriate conditions for your platform
- [ ] **Equipment inspection** - No damage, all components secure
- [ ] **Battery check** - Proper charge level, no damage/swelling
- [ ] **Area survey** - Clear of people, animals, obstacles
- [ ] **Communication check** - Radio link, telemetry working
- [ ] **Emergency plan** - Know what to do if something goes wrong

### During Operation

- [ ] **Maintain awareness** - Never operate distracted
- [ ] **Respect limits** - Don't exceed your skill level
- [ ] **Monitor battery** - Land/return with reserve power
- [ ] **Watch for hazards** - People, animals, vehicles approaching
- [ ] **Stay in control** - Abort operation if uncomfortable
- [ ] **Use spotter** - Second person helps situational awareness

### After Operation

- [ ] **Safe shutdown** - Proper power-off sequence
- [ ] **Battery care** - Immediate storage procedures
- [ ] **Equipment check** - Inspect for damage
- [ ] **Area cleanup** - Remove all equipment and debris
- [ ] **Log flight/operation** - Track hours, issues, maintenance

## Risk Assessment Matrix

Evaluate risk for each operation:

| Risk Level | Description | Example | Action Required |
|------------|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| **Low** | Controlled environment, minimal consequences | Indoor micro drone, experienced operator | Standard procedures |
| **Medium** | Some uncontrolled factors, moderate consequences | Outdoor flight in park, novice operator | Enhanced precautions, spotter |
| **High** | Multiple risk factors, serious consequences possible | Near people, windy conditions, new platform | Comprehensive safety plan, multiple spotters |
| **Unacceptable** | Extreme danger, major consequences likely | Over crowds, extreme weather, unapproved area | **DO NOT OPERATE** |

!!! warning "When in Doubt, Don't Fly/Drive"
    If you're uncertain about safety, **postpone the operation**. It's always better to wait for better conditions than to risk an incident.

## Domain-Specific Considerations

### 🚁 Aerial (UAVs/Drones)

**Unique Hazards:**
- Falls from height (can cause serious injury)
- Spinning propellers (lacerations)
- Airspace conflicts with manned aircraft
- Wind and weather vulnerabilities

**Extra Precautions:**
- Propeller guards for beginners
- Visual observers (spotters)
- Altitude and distance limits
- No-fly zone awareness

---

### 🚗 Ground (UGVs/Rovers)

**Unique Hazards:**
- Collision with obstacles/people
- Pinch points in wheels/tracks
- Trip hazards (cables, following person)
- Environmental damage (vegetation, wildlife)

**Extra Precautions:**
- Bright colors/flags for visibility
- Speed limits in populated areas
- E-stop (emergency stop) readily accessible
- Boundary/geofencing for autonomous operation

---

### 🚤 Marine (USVs/Boats)

**Unique Hazards:**
- Water damage to electronics
- Boat traffic and waterway rules
- Loss of platform (sinking, drifting)
- Saltwater corrosion (if applicable)

**Extra Precautions:**
- Flotation devices on platform
- Bright colors for visibility
- Marine radio monitoring (if required)
- Plan for retrieval if lost
- Rinse after saltwater use

## Emergency Procedures Quick Reference

### Battery Fire
1. **Alert others** - Shout "FIRE!"
2. **Evacuate** - Move people away (20+ feet)
3. **Use appropriate extinguisher** - ABC or metal fire (NOT water)
4. **If safe:** Move to outdoor area or metal container
5. **Call 911** - For significant fires

**Detailed procedures:** [Battery Safety](battery-safety.md#emergency-response)

---

### Flyaway (Aerial)

1. **Alert area** - Warn people in flight path
2. **Attempt return-to-home** - If configured
3. **Cut throttle** - If over safe area
4. **Track direction** - Note where it goes
5. **Report if required** - FAA for certain incidents

---

### Loss of Control

1. **Emergency stop** - Use kill switch/disarm
2. **Clear hazards** - Move people away
3. **Assess situation** - Is it safe to approach?
4. **Safe recovery** - Disconnect battery first
5. **Investigate cause** - Before next operation

---

### Injury

1. **Stop operation immediately**
2. **Assess injury severity**
3. **Call for help** - 911 for serious injuries
4. **Render first aid** - If trained
5. **Document incident** - For reporting and learning

## Legal and Liability

### Personal Liability

- **You are responsible** for your vehicle's actions
- **Damage to property** - You may be liable for repairs
- **Injury to persons** - Serious legal and financial consequences
- **Insurance** - Consider hobby/commercial liability insurance

### Criminal Penalties

Serious violations can result in:

- **Fines** - FAA violations up to $27,500 (civil) or $250,000 (criminal)
- **Imprisonment** - Criminal reckless operation
- **Equipment confiscation** - Illegal operations
- **No-fly restrictions** - Future bans

**Compliance is not optional.**

## Safety Culture

Building a culture of safety:

### Individual Responsibility
- **Take safety seriously** - It's not a hassle, it's essential
- **Continuous learning** - Stay updated on best practices
- **Speak up** - Report unsafe practices
- **Lead by example** - Especially when teaching others

### Team/Group Safety
- **Buddy system** - Operate with others when possible
- **Peer review** - Check each other's procedures
- **Share lessons learned** - Discuss near-misses and incidents
- **No blame** - Focus on learning, not punishment (for honest mistakes)

### Institutional Safety
- **Clear policies** - Document and communicate rules
- **Regular training** - Annual refreshers minimum
- **Safety equipment** - Provide PPE, fire safety, first aid
- **Incident review** - Analyze and prevent recurrence
