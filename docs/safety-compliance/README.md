Welcome to the Safety & Compliance section - **the most important section in this repository**.

## Purpose

This section provides comprehensive guidance on safety protocols, regulatory compliance, risk management, and emergency procedures for operating unmanned systems in educational environments.

## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST

Operating unmanned systems involves real risks including:
- Personal injury from propellers and moving parts
- Property damage
- Regulatory violations and fines
- Liability issues
- Privacy concerns

**Never skip safety protocols or regulatory requirements.**

## Key Regulatory Topics

### United States (FAA)

**Part 107 - Commercial Operations**
- Required for most school operations
- Remote pilot certificate required
- Operational limitations (daylight, VLOS, etc.)
- Waiver process for special operations

**44809 - Recreational Exception**
- Limited applicability to schools
- CBO (Community Based Organization) requirements
- More restrictive than Part 107

**Educational Exemptions**
- Some activities may qualify for exemptions
- Requires proper documentation
- Consult with legal counsel

### International

- **Canada** - Transport Canada regulations
- **Europe** - EASA drone regulations
- **UK** - CAA drone code
- **Australia** - CASA rules
- **Other regions** - Consult local authorities

## Essential Safety Protocols

### Before Every Flight

1. **Risk Assessment** - Complete for each activity
2. **Weather Check** - Wind, rain, visibility
3. **Airspace Check** - No-fly zones, temporary restrictions
4. **Equipment Check** - Pre-flight inspection
5. **Safety Briefing** - All participants informed
6. **Safety Zone** - Established and marked
7. **Emergency Plan** - Reviewed and ready

### During Operations

- Maintain visual line of sight (VLOS)
- Keep spectators at safe distance (typically 30+ feet)
- Monitor battery levels
- Watch for weather changes
- Have safety observer
- Stay below altitude limits

### After Operations

- Post-flight inspection
- Log any issues
- Secure equipment
- Debrief any incidents
- Document lessons learned

## Educational Setting Requirements

### Supervision Ratios

Recommended ratios for hands-on activities:
- **Elementary (K-5)**: 1:5 (one adult per 5 students)
- **Middle School (6-8)**: 1:8
- **High School (9-12)**: 1:10 (experienced students)

### Safety Equipment

- **Personal Protective Equipment** - Safety glasses required
- **First Aid Kit** - Readily accessible
- **Fire Extinguisher** - For LiPo battery fires (Class D)
- **Communication** - Radio or phone for emergencies

### Facility Requirements

- Designated flying area away from people
- Clear of obstacles and hazards
- Weather protection (wind breaks)
- Storage for equipment
- Battery charging station (fireproof)

## Risk Management

### Risk Assessment Matrix

| Likelihood | Severity | Risk Level | Action |
|-----------|----------|------------|--------|
| High | High | Extreme | Do not proceed |
| High | Medium | High | Additional controls required |
| Medium | Medium | Medium | Proceed with caution |
| Low | Low | Low | Standard protocols |

### Common Hazards

- **Propeller strikes** - Most common injury source
- **Battery fires** - LiPo battery hazard
- **Loss of control** - Equipment malfunction
- **Weather** - Wind, rain affecting control
- **Collision** - With obstacles or aircraft

## Insurance Considerations

Schools should maintain:
- General liability insurance
- Specific unmanned systems coverage
- Personal injury coverage
- Property damage coverage

**Consult with your school's insurance provider before starting a program.**

## Emergency Contacts

### In Case of Emergency

1. **Injury** - Call 911 / local emergency services
2. **School Administration** - Notify immediately
3. **FAA** (for aviation incidents) - 1-866-TELL-FAA
4. **Insurance Provider** - Report incidents

### Incident Reporting

Document all incidents and near-misses:
- Date, time, location
- Weather conditions
- What happened
- Contributing factors
- Injuries or damage
- Corrective actions
