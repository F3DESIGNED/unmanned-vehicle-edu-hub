# UAV Systems

Welcome to the UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) Systems section, your comprehensive resource for aerial platforms in education.

## Purpose

This section covers all aspects of UAV systems including quadcopters, fixed-wing aircraft, racing drones, and specialized platforms. From beginner-friendly builds to advanced competition-ready systems.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Platform Comparisons** - ArduPilot vs Betaflight vs INAV
- **Build Guides** - Complete step-by-step instructions for multiple platforms
- **Flight Training** - From first hover to advanced maneuvers
- **Configuration** - Setup and tuning guides
- **Competition Prep** - Racing and autonomous challenge preparation

## Platforms Covered

- **ArduPilot** - Advanced autonomous capabilities, GPS missions
- **Betaflight** - High-performance racing and acrobatics
- **INAV** - Navigation-focused for long range and waypoints
- **Cleanflight** - Lightweight and simple
- **Custom Solutions** - Navio2 + Raspberry Pi

## Skill Level Guide

- **Beginner**: RTF (Ready-to-Fly) and simple kit builds
- **Intermediate**: Custom builds with GPS and telemetry
- **Advanced**: Racing drones, custom firmware, autonomous missions

## Safety Requirements

**All UAV operations require:**
- Understanding of local regulations (FAA Part 107 in US)
- Proper risk assessment
- Designated flying area away from people
- Visual observer and safety zone
- Emergency procedures

See [Safety & Compliance](../safety-compliance/) for complete information.
