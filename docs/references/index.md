- **Motors**: Specifications, performance curves, compatibility
- **ESCs**: Protocols, firmware, configuration
- **Flight Controllers**: Pinouts, parameters, capabilities
- **Sensors**: GPS, IMU, barometer, compass specifications
- **Batteries**: Chemistry, discharge rates, safety

### Protocols and Standards
- **MAVLink**: Message definitions, implementations
- **MSP**: Multiwii Serial Protocol documentation
- **DroneCAN**: CAN bus communication
- **I2C/SPI**: Sensor interfaces
- **PWM/DShot**: Motor control protocols

## Educational Resources

### Recommended Textbooks

**Introductory (Grades 6-10)**
- "Drones: From Insect Spy Drones to Bomber Drones" by Sarah Levete
- "Make: Drones" by David McGriffy

**Advanced (Grades 11+)**
- "Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots" by Siegwart & Nourbakhsh
- "Small Unmanned Aircraft" by Beard & McLain
- "Fundamentals of Small Unmanned Aircraft Flight" by Gundlach

**Teachers**
- "Teaching with Drones" by Patrick O'Shea
- "Project-Based Learning with Drones" (various authors)

### Online Courses
- Coursera: Aerial Robotics
- edX: Autonomous Mobile Robots
- Udemy: Complete Guide to Building Drones
- Khan Academy: Physics and Mathematics foundations

### Video Resources
- Joshua Bardwell (FPV and flight controllers)
- Painless360 (ArduPilot tutorials)
- Oscar Liang (Build guides and reviews)
- Nick Rehm (Advanced control theory)
