# Administrative Advocacy Toolkit

Resources for teachers to pitch, justify, and gain administrative support for drone programs.

## Quick Pitch Materials

### 30-Second Elevator Pitch
"Our students need skills in robotics, coding, and autonomous systems—exactly what drone technology teaches. For less than the cost of one traditional lab upgrade, we can launch a program that engages students, meets state standards, and connects directly to growing career opportunities. I'd love to share our plan with you."

### Key Selling Points
- **Student Engagement**: Hands-on, project-based learning
- **Career Connection**: Direct path to high-paying jobs
- **Cost-Effective**: Lower cost than traditional labs
- **Standards-Aligned**: Meets STEM/CTE requirements
- **Safety Record**: Strong safety protocols
- **Community Impact**: Service learning opportunities

## Getting Started

1. Review [elevator pitch](./pitch-materials/elevator-pitch-template.md)
2. Prepare [one-page overview](./pitch-materials/one-page-overview-template.md)
3. Anticipate concerns using [FAQ](./addressing-concerns/faq-for-administrators.md)
4. Request 15-minute meeting with administrator
5. Follow up with [pilot proposal](./pilot-program-proposal/proposal-template.md)
