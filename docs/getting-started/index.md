## Common Questions

??? question "How much will this cost?"

    Costs vary widely based on platform choice:

    - **Entry Level (RTF):** $75-$200
    - **Intermediate (BNF/ARF):** $200-$500
    - **Advanced (Custom):** $500-$1500+

    See [Platform Selection Guide](selecting-your-platform.md) for detailed breakdowns.

??? question "Do I need programming experience?"

    It depends on your goals:

    - **Level 1-2:** No programming required
    - **Level 3:** Basic understanding helpful
    - **Level 4:** Programming experience recommended

    See [Prerequisites](prerequisites.md) for skill details.

??? question "Is this legal?"

    Legality depends on your location and platform:

    - **USA:** Review [FAA Regulations](../safety-compliance/faa-regulations.md)
    - **Schools:** Check [School Policies](../safety-compliance/school-policies.md)
    - **Other countries:** Research local aviation/robotics laws

    Always prioritize safety and legal compliance!

??? question "What's the best platform for beginners?"

    Most common recommendations:

    - **Aerial:** DJI Tello or Ryze Tello ($100) - perfect for learning
    - **Ground:** Arduino-based rover kit ($75-150) - great intro to robotics
    - **Marine:** Modified RC boat ($100-200) - unique challenge

    See detailed analysis in [Platform Selection](selecting-your-platform.md).

??? question "How long until I can fly/drive/sail?"

    Timeline by build level:

    - **Level 1 (RTF):** Same day - 30 minutes to 2 hours
    - **Level 2 (BNF):** 1-2 days - includes radio setup
    - **Level 3 (ARF):** 1-2 weeks - includes assembly and testing
    - **Level 4 (Custom):** 2-8 weeks - full design and build

    These assume proper preparation and no major issues.

## Safety Reminder

!!! danger "Before You Start"

    Unmanned vehicles can be dangerous if not handled properly. Before proceeding:

    - [ ] Read all safety documentation
    - [ ] Understand LiPo battery risks
    - [ ] Know local regulations
    - [ ] Have appropriate safety equipment
    - [ ] Plan a safe testing environment

    **Your safety and the safety of others depends on your preparation!**
