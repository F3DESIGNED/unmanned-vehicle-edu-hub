## 🎯 Platform Selection Guide

### Decision Factors

Not sure which platform to choose? Consider these factors:

#### 1. Student Age & Skill Level

| Age Group | Recommended Starting Platform | Why? |
|-----------|------------------------------|------|
| **Elementary (K-5)** | Pre-built drones with safety features | Focus on concepts, not building |
| **Middle School (6-8)** | Arduino UGV or RTF quadcopter kits | Structured builds, moderate complexity |
| **High School (9-12)** | ArduPilot systems or racing drones | Room to grow, competition-ready |
| **Post-Secondary** | Custom builds, research platforms | Maximum flexibility |

#### 2. Budget Tiers

| Budget | What You Can Build | Recommended Path |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Under $100** | Basic Arduino UGV, simple sensors | [Arduino Ground Vehicle Guide](ugv-systems/) |
| **$100-$300** | RTF quadcopter or basic custom build | [Budget UAV Build](uav-systems/) |
| **$300-$500** | ArduPilot with GPS, telemetry | [ArduPilot Setup Guide](uav-systems/) |
| **$500+** | Racing drone, advanced sensors, multiple vehicles | [Advanced Builds](uav-systems/) |

**Budget-saving tips:** See [Bill of Materials](../hardware/bill-of-materials/) for cost-effective component sourcing

#### 3. Learning Objectives

| Learning Goal | Best Platform | Key Skills Developed |
|--------------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Programming Basics** | Arduino UGV | C++, sensors, logic |
| **Physics & Aerodynamics** | Fixed-wing UAV | Forces, stability, design |
| **Control Systems** | Quadcopter | PID tuning, stabilization |
| **Autonomous Systems** | ArduPilot + Raspberry Pi | Mission planning, AI, computer vision |
| **Competition Prep** | Racing drone or BattleBots | Advanced flying, strategy, engineering |

#### 4. Available Infrastructure

**Indoor Space Only?**
- Focus on UGV systems
- Micro/nano drones (< 50g)
- Simulation software

**Outdoor Access?**
- Full-size quadcopters
- Fixed-wing aircraft
- Long-range missions

**No Flying Zone?**
- UGV exclusive programs
- Drone simulators
- Ground-based robotics

## 🛡️ Safety Notice

### READ THIS BEFORE BEGINNING

Operating unmanned systems involves real risks. Before starting any project:

1. **Review Safety Documentation** - Read [Safety & Compliance](safety-compliance/) thoroughly
2. **Understand Regulations** - Know your local laws (FAA Part 107 in the US)
3. **Assess Risks** - Complete risk assessments for all activities
4. **Ensure Supervision** - Maintain appropriate adult supervision ratios
5. **Use PPE** - Safety glasses, appropriate clothing
6. **Prepare for Emergencies** - Have emergency procedures in place

**Key Safety Principles:**

- **Never fly near people** without proper authorization
- **Always maintain visual line of sight**
- **Respect no-fly zones** (airports, stadiums, etc.)
- **Test in safe environments** before classroom demonstrations
- **Have proper insurance** coverage
- **Document incidents** and near-misses for learning

### Educational Exemptions

Some educational uses may qualify for regulatory exemptions or simplified compliance. See [Safety & Compliance](safety-compliance/) for details specific to your location.
