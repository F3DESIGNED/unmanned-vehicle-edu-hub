# Curriculum Integration

This section provides resources for integrating unmanned vehicle systems into educational curricula across subjects and grade levels. Find standards-aligned lesson plans, project-based units, and assessment tools ready for classroom use.

## Overview

Unmanned vehicle systems offer rich opportunities for integrated STEM education:

- **Science**: Aerodynamics, forces, energy, sensors, environment
- **Technology**: Programming, electronics, systems integration
- **Engineering**: Design process, prototyping, testing, optimization
- **Mathematics**: Trigonometry, coordinates, calculus, statistics

## Implementation Models

### Stand-Alone Course
**Full Semester (18 weeks)**

- Weeks 1-3: Safety and fundamentals
- Weeks 4-8: Build and configuration
- Weeks 9-12: Flight training and programming
- Weeks 13-16: Student projects
- Weeks 17-18: Presentations and assessment

### Integrated Unit
**4-6 Weeks within Existing Course**

- Physics: Forces and motion unit with drone labs
- Math: Coordinate geometry with navigation
- Computer Science: Robotics programming unit
- Engineering: Design process with vehicle project

### After-School Program
**12-Week Club Format**

- Focus on hands-on building and flying
- Competition preparation
- Student-led projects
- Community outreach

### Special Topics
**1-2 Week Intensive**

- Spring break program
- Summer camp
- Weekend workshop series
- Professional development for teachers

## Learning Progressions

### Elementary (Grades 3-5)
**Focus**: Basic concepts, observation, wonder

- What are drones? Introduction
- Simple flying demonstrations
- Design paper/foam gliders
- Basic programming concepts (block-based)

### Middle School (Grades 6-8)
**Focus**: Hands-on exploration, foundational skills

- Physics of flight
- Build simple quadcopters
- Introduction to flight control
- Basic autonomous programming
- Safety certification

### High School (Grades 9-12)
**Focus**: Advanced projects, career preparation

- Complete UAV/UGV systems
- Advanced programming (Python, C++)
- Mission planning and autonomy
- Sensor integration
- Industry-standard tools
- Competition teams

### Post-Secondary
**Focus**: Professional skills, specialization

- Advanced autonomous systems
- Sensor fusion and AI
- Commercial applications
- Regulatory compliance
- Capstone projects

## Differentiation Strategies

### For Advanced Students
- Leadership roles in team projects
- Research extensions
- Competition participation
- Peer tutoring opportunities
- Advanced programming challenges

### For Struggling Students
- Scaffold assignments
- More structured build guides
- Partner with stronger student
- Focus on specific subsystems
- Simulator practice before hardware

### For Special Needs
- Adaptive controllers
- Visual instructions
- Simulator-first approach
- Role assignment in teams
- Focus on individual strengths

## Safety and Compliance

All curriculum materials emphasize:

- **Safety First**: Comprehensive safety training before hands-on
- **Regulatory Compliance**: FAA rules, local regulations
- **Risk Management**: Insurance, waivers, supervision
- **Ethical Use**: Privacy, responsible innovation

See [Safety & Compliance](../safety-compliance/index.md) for complete procedures.
