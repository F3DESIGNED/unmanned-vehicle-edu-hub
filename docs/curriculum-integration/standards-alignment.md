# Standards Alignment

Comprehensive mapping of unmanned vehicle systems curriculum to national and state educational standards.

## Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)

### High School Physical Sciences

**HS-PS2 Motion and Stability: Forces and Interactions**

| Standard | Application | Lesson/Activity |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| HS-PS2-1 | Analyze data to support Newton's second law of motion | Forces in Flight lesson, thrust testing |
| HS-PS2-2 | Use mathematical representations of Newton's Law of Gravitation | GPS navigation, orbital mechanics (advanced) |
| HS-PS2-3 | Apply scientific and engineering ideas to design systems | Frame design, propulsion optimization |

**HS-PS3 Energy**

| Standard | Application | Lesson/Activity |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| HS-PS3-1 | Create computational model to calculate energy changes | Battery capacity vs flight time |
| HS-PS3-2 | Develop and use models to illustrate energy transfer | Motor efficiency, electrical to kinetic energy |
| HS-PS3-3 | Design and evaluate devices that minimize/maximize thermal energy | ESC cooling, motor heat management |

**HS-ETS1 Engineering Design**

| Standard | Application | Lesson/Activity |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| HS-ETS1-1 | Analyze major global challenge | Search and rescue, environmental monitoring |
| HS-ETS1-2 | Design solution to complex problem | Autonomous delivery project |
| HS-ETS1-3 | Evaluate solution using computer simulations | Flight simulation, mission planning |
| HS-ETS1-4 | Use computer simulation to model testing | Gazebo/SITL simulation |

## Career and Technical Education (CTE) Standards

### Transportation, Distribution & Logistics

- **Aircraft Systems**: Flight control systems, autopilot, navigation
- **Aerospace Engineering**: Design, testing, performance optimization
- **Aviation Maintenance**: Inspection, repair, component replacement

### STEM

- **Engineering & Technology**: Design process, prototyping, testing
- **Computer Science**: Programming, algorithms, sensor integration
- **Applied Mathematics**: Calculations for flight dynamics, navigation

### Information Technology

- **Programming & Software Development**: Python, C++, flight controller firmware
- **Network Systems**: MAVLink protocol, telemetry, ground stations
- **Cybersecurity**: Secure communication, authentication (advanced)

## State-Specific Examples

**NYS P-12 Science Learning Standards**: Physical sciences, engineering design
**CTE Technical Standards**: Engineering & Technologies pathway
