# Responsible Operating Guidelines

## Safety First
- Pre-flight checks
- Weather assessment
- Emergency procedures
- Maintenance schedules

## Privacy Respect
- Avoid flying over private property when possible
- Don't intentionally capture private activities
- Secure data appropriately
- Respect requests to cease operations

## Environmental Responsibility
- Minimize flights to necessary missions
- Avoid sensitive habitats
- Proper battery disposal
- Noise awareness

## Community Relations
- Communicate with neighbors
- Follow local ordinances beyond FAA rules
- Address concerns promptly
- Educate about benefits

## Professional Conduct
- Maintain certifications
- Follow regulations
- Continuous learning
- Ethical decision-making
