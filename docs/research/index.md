# Research Framework - UVS Education Hub

## Overview

The UVS Education Hub is committed to building rigorous evidence of program effectiveness and contributing to the broader field of STEM education research. This research framework guides evaluation, supports research partnerships, and ensures ethical, high-quality inquiry.

---

## Purpose

This research framework serves to:
- **Establish** research agenda and priorities
- **Guide** program evaluation and improvement
- **Support** research partnerships and collaborations
- **Ensure** ethical and rigorous research practices
- **Build** evidence base for unmanned vehicle systems education
- **Disseminate** findings to advance the field

---

## Research Agenda

**Priority research questions:**

### Student Learning and Development
- What do students learn through the UVS curriculum?
- How does participation affect STEM interest, self-efficacy, and career aspirations?
- What factors predict student success?
- How do learning outcomes vary by student characteristics and contexts?

### Implementation and Scale
- What does high-quality implementation look like?
- What supports effective implementation?
- How can the program scale while maintaining quality?
- What adaptations are effective in different contexts?

### Equity and Access
- How can the program effectively serve underrepresented students in STEM?
- What barriers to participation exist and how can they be addressed?
- Does the program reduce or exacerbate achievement gaps?

### Teacher Professional Development
- How do teachers develop content knowledge and pedagogical skills?
- What professional development structures are most effective?
- How does teaching UVS curriculum affect broader teaching practice?

**See [Research Agenda](research-agenda.md) for detailed questions and hypotheses.**

## Ethical Research Practices

### Human Subjects Protection

**All research must:**
- Obtain IRB approval
- Protect participant privacy and confidentiality
- Use informed consent/assent appropriately
- Minimize risk and burden
- Ensure FERPA compliance

**See [Pilot Program IRB Guidance](../pilot-program/research-partnership/irb-guidance.md)**

---

### Data Ethics

**Principles:**
- Collect only necessary data
- Store and transmit securely
- De-identify when possible
- Respect participant wishes (opt-out, withdrawal)
- Use data only for approved purposes
- Share appropriately and responsibly

---

### Research Integrity

**Commitments:**
- Honest and accurate reporting
- Transparent about methods and limitations
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Proper attribution and citation
- Responsible authorship
- Respect intellectual property

## Evaluation Framework

### Program Evaluation Approach

**Logic model:**
- **Inputs:** Curriculum, PD, support, resources
- **Activities:** Teaching and learning, implementation
- **Outputs:** Students served, hours of instruction, fidelity
- **Outcomes:** Knowledge, skills, attitudes, engagement, aspirations
- **Impact:** Career pathways, educational attainment, workforce contribution

**Evaluation questions:**
- **Implementation:** Is program implemented as intended? What adaptations?
- **Effectiveness:** Does program achieve intended outcomes?
- **Cost-effectiveness:** Outcomes relative to resources?
- **Scalability:** Can it scale? Under what conditions?
- **Sustainability:** Can schools sustain it? What's needed?

---

### Evaluation Timeline

**Year 1 (Cohort 1):**
- Formative evaluation for rapid improvement
- Pre-post student outcomes
- Implementation documentation
- Preliminary impact evidence

**Year 2-3:**
- Summative evaluation with multiple cohorts
- Comparative studies across contexts
- Cost-effectiveness analysis
- Longitudinal tracking (where feasible)

**Ongoing:**
- Continuous improvement cycle
- Annual evaluation reports
- Research publications
- Meta-analyses as field grows
