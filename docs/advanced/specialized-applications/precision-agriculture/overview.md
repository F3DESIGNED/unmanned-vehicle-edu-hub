# Precision Agriculture with Drones

## Industry Overview

Precision agriculture uses technology to optimize crop production, reduce waste, and increase sustainability. Drones are transforming agriculture by providing detailed crop health data at a fraction of traditional costs.

## Market Opportunity

- **Global Market**: $8+ billion and growing rapidly
- **Job Growth**: High demand for ag technology specialists
- **Applications**: Crop monitoring, spraying, seeding, livestock management
- **ROI**: Typical 5-10x return on investment for farmers

## Technical Applications

### Crop Health Monitoring
- Multispectral/hyperspectral imaging
- Vegetation indices (NDVI, NDRE)
- Disease and stress detection
- Growth stage assessment

### Precision Spraying
- Variable rate application
- Spot treatment of weeds/pests
- Reduced chemical usage (up to 90%)
- Automated flight planning

### Yield Estimation
- Crop counting and measurement
- Biomass estimation
- Harvest timing optimization
- Historical yield mapping

### Field Mapping
- Topographic surveys
- Drainage analysis
- Soil sampling guidance
- Planting zone delineation

## Required Knowledge

- Remote sensing principles
- Plant biology basics
- Agricultural practices
- Data analysis and GIS
- Regulatory compliance (pesticide application)

## Equipment Needs

### Minimum
- Drone with RGB camera
- GPS for georeferencing
- Basic image processing software

### Recommended
- Multispectral camera
- RTK GPS for precision
- Specialized ag software (Pix4D, DroneDeploy)
- Ground control points

### Professional
- Fixed-wing drone for large areas
- Hyperspectral sensors
- Thermal cameras
- Spraying equipment
