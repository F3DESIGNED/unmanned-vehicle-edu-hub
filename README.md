> Comprehensive open-source resources for implementing UAV and UGV systems in K-12 STEM and CTE education

## 🎯 Mission

Empower educators and students with accessible, standards-aligned resources for hands-on learning through unmanned vehicle systems. From basic remote control to advanced autonomous operations, this hub supports complete learning pathways in robotics, programming, and engineering.

## 🚁 What's Inside

- **Complete Build Guides** for UAVs and UGVs at multiple skill levels
- **Programming Resources** for Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ArduPilot, Betaflight, INAV, and more
- **Curriculum Integration** with lesson plans aligned to NGSS, Common Core, and CTE standards
- **Safety & Compliance** guidance for educational settings
- **Troubleshooting** resources and technical support documentation
- **Community Contributions** from educators worldwide

## 🎓 Educational Levels

- **Elementary (K-5)**: Basic concepts, supervised builds, safety awareness
- **Middle School (6-8)**: Structured builds, block programming, flight training
- **High School (9-12)**: Advanced programming, autonomous systems, competition prep
- **Post-Secondary**: Research applications, custom development, industry preparation

## 🛠️ Supported Platforms

### Flight Controllers
- ArduPilot (Pixhawk family, Cube, Holybro)
- Betaflight, INAV, CleanFlight
- Navio2 + Raspberry Pi

### Embedded Systems
- Arduino (Uno, Mega, Nano, ESP32)
- Raspberry Pi (3B+, 4, Zero 2 W)

### Communication Protocols
- MAVLink, MSP, ROS/ROS2
- SBUS, PPM, IBUS

## ⚖️ Safety & Legal

**Important:** Operating unmanned systems requires compliance with local regulations. In the US, consult [FAA Part 107](link) and educational exceptions under [44809](link). Always prioritize safety in educational settings.

See [Safety & Compliance Guide](docs/safety-compliance/) for complete information.
